## Section 1 — Purpose and authority

### Article 1-1 Purpose

The zoning regulations and districts as set forth herein have been made in accordance with the General Plan for Land Use and Major Streets which was prepared in order to promote, in accordance with present and future needs, the safety, order. convenience, and general welfare of the citizens of Rector, Arkansas. This ordinance has been designed to lessen congestion in the streets, to secure safety from fire and other danger, to prevent the overcrowding of land and facilitate the adequate provision of water, sewerage, schools, parks, and other public improvements.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 1-1](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 1-2 Authority

Act 186 enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Arkansas in 1957 provides the City of Rector, under guidance of the Planning Commission, with the authority to adopt, administer, enforce, and amend these regulations. These regulations are in effect within the entire City limits of Rector.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 1-2](/ordinances/359).*

## Section 2 — Definitions

### Article 2-1 Definitions

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 2-1](/ordinances/359); except definitions separately cited below.*

1. Words used in the present tense include the future; words in the singular include the plural number, and words in the plural include the singular; the work "shall" is mandatory and not directory.

1. Certain words and phrases shall, for the purpose of this ordinance, have the following definitions:

      **Accessory Structure.** A subordinate structure located on the same lot with the principal structure. Where an accessory structure is attached to the principal structure in a substantial manner, as by a roof, such accessory structure shall be considered as a part of the principal structure.

      **Accessory Use.** A use which is customarily incidental to the principal use, as a garage for the storage of an automobile by occupant of a residence.

      **Alley.** A minor public way not over 20 feet in width.

      **Area.** The amount of land surface in a lot or parcel of land.

      **Area Requirements.** The yard, lot area, width of lot, height of structure, and parking requirements as set forth for a specific zone in this ordinance.

      **Building.** Any structure having a roof supported by columns or walls, used or intended for use for the shelter or enclosure of persons or property.

      **Community Development Plan.** The official plan for future land use, streets, and community facilities, as adopted by the City of Rector, Clay County, Arkansas.

      **District Zoning.** A portion of the City Rector, within which certain uniform regulations and requirements apply under the provisions of these regulations.

      **Dwelling Unit.** A unit intended to be occupied as a housekeeping unit.

      **Enforcement Officer.** The persons officially appointed by the City Council to enforce the provisions of these regulations.

      **Family.** One or more persons occupying premises and living as a single, non-profit housekeeping unit.

      **Flood Plain.** The area adjoining a river, stream or body of water which is inundated by a regulatory flood. That portion of the flood plain beyond the limits of the floodway (flood fringe) is subject to less frequent and lower velocity flooding. This portion of the flood plain does not play a major role in passing flood flows and may be developed, provided appropriate flood protection measures are intitiated.

      **Floodway.** The channel of a stream or body of water and those portions of the adjoining flood plains which are reasonably required to carry and discharge flood water or flood flow of a designated magnitude (100 year flood) and carry the higher velocity-high hazard water flows. Should not be considered for any type of structural development.

      **Home Occupation.** A use which: (1) is carried on in a dwelling unit or in a building accessory to a dwelling unit, and (2) is clearly incidental and secondary to the residential use of the dwelling. The term "home occupation" includes: (1) custom dressmaking, millinery, tailoring, sewing, barber or beauty shop, (2) fabrication and/or assembly of handicraft articles, not involving heating, refining, chemical, or similar processes, (3) fine arts studio in which are created only individual works of art, (4) professional office of an accountant, architect, attorney, author, engineer, or other similar professional persons but not including medical, dental, osteopathic, or chiropractic offices, (5) teaching or tutoring, including music, dancing, speech, or other arts with instruction limited to one person at a time. The term "home occupation" shall not be interpreted to include: (1) Commercial stable or kennel, (2) sales to customers except products made on the premises, (3) veterinary surgeon, (4) commercial repairing of automobiles, appliances, equipment, or similar items. Home occupations are subject to all the following conditions:

      (1) in any dwelling unit, the total of all home occupations shall not occupy more than 25 percent of the gross floor area of one floor, (2) a home occupation shall not require internal or external alterations of construction of the dwelling, (3) there shall not be displayed or created outside the building any external evidence of the operation of the occupation except that one unanimated, non-illuminated, accessory identification sign not exceeding an area of two square feet may be displayed flat against a wall or door or in a window, (4) power shall be limited to electric motors with a total limitation of three horsepower, (5) the home occupation shall be conducted solely by residents of the dwelling and no employees who live outside the dwelling shall be used, and (6) there shall be no retail sales to customers except products made on the premises by the head of household or members of his immediate family. Only one home occupation shall be permitted in a residential structure or portion thereof.

      **Lot.** Land occupied or to be occupied by a structure or use and its accessory structure and uses, and including such open spaces as are required under these regulations and having public street frontage.

      **Lot Depth.** The mean horizontal distance between the front lot line and the rear lot line of a lot.

      **Lot, Exterior.** A lot abutting on two or more streets at their intersection.

      **Lot, Interior.** Any lot that is not an exterior lot.

      **Manufactured Home.** Manufactured homes means a dwelling unit manufactured in accordance with the provisions set forth in 24CFR part 3280-Manufactured Home Construction and Safety Standards as amended October 25, 1994, and installed in accordance with applicable state regulations.

      *Source: [Ordinance 436, § 2](/ordinances/436); replacing the definition in [Ordinance 372, § 2, Article 2-1(B)](/ordinances/372).*

      **Mobile Home** means a transportable structure having an undercarriage of one or more axles and wheels, a hitch mechanism and designed to remain capable of being towed on the highway. Merely placing such mobile home on a permanent foundation does not alter its character so as to make it a manufactured home for the purpose of this Ordinance.

      *Source: [Ordinance 372, § 2, Article 2-1(B)](/ordinances/372).*

      **Mobile Home Park.** A commercial park developed for the purpose of offering for rent or lease spaces for the location of mobile homes. Such parks are equipped with utility connections, parking pads, and driveways on each space, minimal interior street systems, and if designed for twenty-five (25) or more mobile homes shall include a recreation area, laundry and bathing facilities, and other amenities as determined by the Planning Commission.

      **Non-Conforming.** That use or structure which does not meet the requirements of the zone in which the use or structure is located at the time of the effective date of this ordinance.

      **Non-Conforming Use of Land.** That use of land which does not conform to the permitted uses of land in the zone in which it is located and which does not utilize a principal or permanent structure.

      **Non-Conforming Use of Structure.** That use of a structure which does not conform to the permitted use of structures of the zone in which it is located.

      **Non-Conforming Structure.** That structure which by its nature is not intended for uses permitted within the zone in which it is located and/or that structure which does not conform to the area requirements of the zone in which it is located.

      **Open Space.** Any unoccupied space on the lot that is open and unobstructed to the sky and occupied by no structure or portion of structures whatever.

      **Parking Space.** 250 square feet of usable and accessible space.

      **Places of Public Assembly.** A meeting place for more than thirty-five (35) persons to which the public or membership groups are assembled regularly or occasionally, including but not limited to schools, churches, theaters, auditoriums, funeral homes, stadiums, and similar places of assembly. Classrooms within a school or schools without auditoriums or gymnasiums are not, for the purpose of this ordinance, considered places of public assembly.

      **Principal Use.** The chief or main recognized use of a structure of land.

      **Property Line.** The line bounding a lot as defined herein.

      **Story.** That portion of a structure included between the upper surface of any floor and the upper surface of the floor next above; also any portion of a structure used for human occupancy between the topmost floor and the roof. A basement shall not be counted as a story unless the height of the surface of the first floor above the average elevation of the finished lot grade at the structure exceeds four (4) feet.

      **Street.** A public way of more than 20 feet established by or maintained under public authority, a private way open for public use, and a private way plotted or laid out for ultimate public use, whether or not constructed.

      **Structure.** Anything constructed or erected with a fixed location on the ground, or attached to something having a fixed location on the ground. Structures include buildings, billboards, and poster panels.

      **Yard.** The horizontal distance from a lot line to a parallel designated line. A yard is an open space and unoccupied space extending the full distance of the lot.

      **Manufactured Housing Construction and Safety Standards Code** means Title VI of the 1974 Housing and Community Development Act (42 U.S.C. 540) et sequentis), as amended (previously known as the federal Mobile Home Construction and Safety Act), rules and regulations adopted thereunder (including information supplied by the home manufacturer, which has been stamped and approved by a Design Approval Primary Inspection Agency, an agent of the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development pursuant to HUD rules), which became effective for mobile/manufactured home construction on June 15, 1976.

      *Source: [Ordinance 372, § 2, Article 2-1(B)](/ordinances/372).*

      **Accessory Dwelling Unit.** A self-contained and independently accessed living unit on the same lot or parcel as a single-family dwelling of greater square footage that includes its own cooking, sleeping, and sanitation facilities and complies with or is otherwise exempt from any applicable regulatory requirements

      **Principal Building.** A building in which the principal use, or one of the principal uses, of a lot or parcel is conducted or which primarily serves such principal use. A building that is subordinate and customarily incidental to a principal use or principal building shall be considered an accessory structure rather than a principal building.

      *Source: [Ordinance 601, § 1](/ordinances/601).*

## Section 3 — Zoning district map and zoning district boundary interpretation

### Article 3-1 Districts Established

The following districts are hereby established:

* A - Agriculture
* R1 - Medium Density Residential
* R2 - High Density Residential
* C1 - Central Business District
* C - General Business
* I - Industrial
{: .usa-list--unstyled}

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 3-1](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 3-2 Zoning Map Established

Such land and the district classification thereof shall be on the map designated as the "Zoning District Map" of the City of Rector, Arkansas, dated and signed by the Mayor.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 3-2](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 3-3 Boundary Interpretation

Interpretation of zoning district boundaries shall include the following:

1. Boundaries indicated as approximately following the center line of highways, streets, roads, alleys or streams shall be construed as following such center lines.
1. Boundaries indicated as approximately following existing property lines, whether or not platted, shall be construed as following such property lines.
1. Boundaries indicated as approximately following City limits shall be construed as following such limits.
1. Boundaries indicated as approximately following section lines or fractional section lines shall be construed to follow such lines.
1. Boundaries of uplatted districts may be shown in lineal feet in which case depth shall be measured from the center line of the fronting street and width shall be measured from the centerline of the siding street or the nearest property boundary.
1. When the street or property layout existing on the ground is at variance with that shown on the zoning map or with other requirements of this ordinance, the Board of Zoning Adjustment shall interpret the boundaries.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 3-3](/ordinances/359).*

## Section 4 — General provisions

There shall be certain provisions which shall apply to all zoning districts within the City of Rector.

### Article 4-1 District Regulations

A district designation of a, R1, R2, C1, C, and I on the Zoning District Map shall mean that the regulations pertaining to that district apply to the entire area within the district boundary lines.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 4-1](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 4-2 Use Restriction

No land or structure may be used except for a purpose permitted in the district in which it is located.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 4-2](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 4-3 Building Compliance Required

Every building or structure hereafter erected, structurally altered, converted, reconstructed, added to, or increased in height shall meet the requirements as set forth for such use in the district in which the building or structure is located.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 4-3](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 4-4 Principal Buildings and Uses

1. **Residential Lots.** Except as otherwise expressly permitted by these regulations or required by state law, no more than one principal building devoted to residential use shall be permitted on a residential lot or parcel.
1. **Accessory Structures and Accessory Dwelling Units.** Accessory buildings and structures permitted by these regulations shall not be considered additional principal buildings for purposes of this Article. An accessory dwelling unit permitted under these regulations or Arkansas law may be located on the same lot or parcel as the principal dwelling and shall not constitute a violation of this Article.
1. **Nonresidential and Institutional Uses.** More than one principal building may be located on a lot or parcel devoted to one or more permitted nonresidential uses when each building is associated with a use permitted on the property. This provision includes, but is not limited to, schools, churches, public facilities, commercial developments, and industrial developments.
1. **Compliance With Other Requirements.** Nothing in this Article shall:
    1. Permit a use that is not otherwise permitted in the zoning district in which the property is located;
    1. Waive or modify any applicable lot area, setback, height, lot coverage, parking, access, building, fire, utility, or other requirement of the City or State of Arkansas;
    1. Allow an accessory structure to be used as a separate principal use unless such use is otherwise permitted by these regulations or required by state law; or
    1. Prevent the enforcement of any other applicable provision of Ordinance No. 359.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 601, § 2](/ordinances/601).*

### Article 4-5 Buildings Prohibited in Floodways

Hereafter, no new building developments of any type shall be permitted in the regulatory floodway (as determined by the U. S. Army Corps of Engineers) of any stream or water body which may cause damaging increases in flood heights or other nuisances (see description of Floodway in Section 2).

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 4-5](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 4-6 Flood Plain Limitations

Hereafter, any new buildings or structures developed in a flood plain which is inundated by a regulatory flood (as determined by the U. S. Army Corps of Engineers) shall: (1) be designed with individual flood protection through elevation or fill, or; (2) be structurally flood proofed to the elevation of the regulatory flood (see description of Flood Plain in Section 2).

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 4-6](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 4-7 Annexed Area

Pending amendment of the Zoning District Map to include newly annexed areas, all applications for building permits shall be referred to the Planning Commission by the enforcement officer.

The Planning Commission or a committee thereof may recommend issuance of a building permit if the intended use conforms to the use for the area as shown on the "General Plan" and the structure meets the requirements of the zoning district in which it will be located.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 4-7](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 4-8 Completion of Existing Buildings

No regulation herein shall require any change in the plans, construction or designated use of a building actually under construction at the time of the adoption of this Ordinance.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 4-8](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 4-9 Home Occupations

After adoption of this Ordinance, undefined Ar 2-1B home occupations shall be prohibited except as approved by the Planning Commission in R-1 and R-2 zones. Defined Home occupations need not obtain Planning Commission approval in R-2 zones.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 4-9](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 4-10 Under-sized Lots

A one-family residential structure may be erected on any lot in the City which is on a plat of record at the time of passage of this Ordinance even though the Lot be of less area or width required under the regulations of the residential zoning district in which the lot is located, provided all other district requirements are met.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 4-10](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 4-11 Areas Not to be Diminished

The lot or yard areas required by this Ordinance for a particular building or use at the time of passage of this Ordinance or later constructed or established shall not be diminished and shall not be included as a part of the required lot, open space, or yard area of any other building or use. If the lot, open space, or yard areas required by this Ordinance for a particular building or use are diminished below requirements, the continued existence of such building or use shall be deemed a violation and punished as provided in this Ordinance.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 4-11](/ordinances/359).*

## Section 5 — Zoning district regulations

### Article 5-1 Agriculture (A)

The purpose of this district is to provide land for agricultural uses, preserve open space for current and future use, conserve soil and water resources, prevent excessive and wasteful scattering of population and settlement, reduce the wastes of an excessive amount of roads and promote the distribution of population to facilitate adequate provision and conservation of community facilities.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 5-1](/ordinances/359).*

#### A. Permitted Uses

1. Residential structures (including mobile homes) of owners and/or operators of agricultural land.

1. Buildings for the storage of farm and associated equipment.

1. Public parks, public playgrounds and recreation areas operated by membership organizations for the benefit of their members and not for gain.

1. Publicly owned and operated properties.

1. Railroad rights-of-ways.

1. Places of worship and associated parish homes.

1. Cemeteries, including mausoleums; providing that any new cemetery shall contain an area of twenty (20) acres or more.

1. Roadside stands, offering for sale only farm products which are produced upon said farm land.

1. Riding stables and veterinary hospitals providing that buildings housing said animals shall be located at least one hundred (100) feet from all property lines.

1. Grain elevators or similar storage structures, including buildings for seasonal or temporary storage of farm products.

1. Hospitals and institutions of an educational, religious, charitable, or philanthropic nature; provided that said buildings shall not be located upon sites containing an area of less than five (5) acres.

1. Accessory building or use customarily incidental to any of the above uses.

1. Public utilities including water and sewage treatment plants, electric distribution plants and solid waste disposal areas.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 5-1(A)](/ordinances/359).*

#### B. Area Requirements

1. Each parcel of land must consist of at least five contiguous acres, excluding all buildings or other structures.

   **Height Regulations.**

1. The maximum height of a structure shall be two stories and not to exceed twenty-four (24) feet.

##### B.

The Planning Commission may waive the height requirements when it is demonstrated that equipment and the structure to house an agricultural operation require greater height.

*Source: [Ordinance 487, Article 5-1(B) amendment](/ordinances/487).*

*Editorial note: Ordinance 487 replaces Article 5-1(B) in full. Its repeated “B.” label is retained; the former parking paragraph is omitted from the replacement.*

1. On-Lot Parking: Places of public assembly shall provide one on-lot parking space per each four persons accommodated in the assembly hall.

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 5-2(D)](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 5-4 Central Business District (C1)

#### A. Permitted Uses

*Source for items 1–6: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 5-4(A)](/ordinances/359).*

1. Retail establishments providing goods and services such as grocery, drug, hardware, variety stores and similar uses, and hotels, motor hotels, restaurants.

1. Office and bank buildings and uses.

1. Bulk storage of non-combustible materials.

### Article 5-7 — Manufactured and Mobile Home Regulations

1. **Purpose** To establish uniform standards for the placement, design, and regulation of manufactured and mobile homes within all zoning districts of the City of Rector, Arkansas, in accordance with the definitions contained in Section 2, Article 2-1 of these regulations.
1. **Permitted Units** Only manufactured homes with valid HUD verification shall be permitted. No manufactured home shall be placed unless it was constructed within twenty (20) years of the date of permit application, as verified by its HUD verification. Mobile homes, as defined by Section 2, Article 2-1 of these regulations, shall be permitted only within approved mobile home parks.
1. **Installation Standards** All manufactured homes must:
    1. Be a minimum of 14 feet in width and 750 square feet in enclosed floor area.
    1. Be supported by a permanent perimeter foundation constructed of concrete or masonry, designed to transfer all loads to the ground, and extending below the frost line. The use of block piers, runners, or other temporary systems shall not satisfy this requirement.
    1. Have all wheels, axles, and towing mechanisms removed.
    1. Have utility hookups (water, sewer, electricity) completed and inspected.
    1. Be roofed with residential-style material and have a minimum roof pitch of 3:12.
    1. Have exterior siding made of vinyl, wood, fiber cement, or other materials customarily used in site-built housing.
    1. Be fully installed, including permanent foundation, removal of wheels and towing apparatus, and utility hookups, within 60 days of the home being delivered to the site.
        1. The Code Enforcement Officer may grant one extension of up to 30 additional days upon written request showing good cause, including weather delays, contractor availability, or other circumstances beyond the applicant’s control.
        1. Failure to complete installation within the required time shall constitute a violation of these regulations and be subject to enforcement under Article 7-3.
1. **Permitting Requirements**
    1. A Certificate of Zoning Compliance and Foundation Permit must be obtained prior to installation.
    1. Permit applications must include:
        1. Site plan with setbacks.
        1. Photographs or elevation drawings.
        1. Foundation details.
        1. A copy of the approved manufacturer instructions, which will be used for installation purposes.
        1. Proof of HUD certification label affixed to the exterior of the home.
1. **Petition Required for Manufactured Home Placement**
    1. All manufactured homes proposed to be placed on a private lot within any zoning district, with the exception of Mobile Home Parks, shall require a petition for placement to be submitted and approved prior to installation.
    1. The petition must include:
        1. A written request to place a manufactured home on the parcel.
        1. Photographs of the proposed unit.
        1. A site plan showing setbacks, access, and placement.
        1. Proof of HUD certification, as defined below.
        1. Written consent from all adjacent property owners, as defined below.
    1. Proof of HUD verification:
        1. Every manufactured home must display an intact HUD verification label affixed to the exterior of the unit.
        1. If the HUD label has been removed, defaced, or is otherwise not visible, the applicant shall provide verification from HUD or the Institute for Building Technology and Safety (IBTS) that the home was constructed in compliance with federal standards.
        1. No manufactured home shall be permitted without either (1) a visible HUD label, or (2) official written verification from HUD/IBTS.
    1. Adjacent property owners shall include all owners of parcels that abut or touch the subject parcel on any side, including across any public street, alley, or right-of-way.
    1. If the petition is complete and compliant with all provisions of this Article, the Code Enforcement Officer may approve the placement.
    1. If the unit does not meet the requirements of this Article, the applicant may seek a variance through the Board of Zoning Adjustment.
    1. No manufactured home shall be installed, delivered, or connected to utilities prior to receiving approval under this section.
1. **Enforcement** Any installation or relocation of a manufactured home in violation of these standards shall be subject to enforcement under Article 7 of these regulations.
1. **Supersession** This article shall govern the regulation of manufactured and mobile homes in all zoning districts. Any other provision of these zoning regulations in conflict with this article is hereby superseded.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 594, § 1](/ordinances/594).*

## Section 6 — Non-conforming

### Article 6-1 Non-conforming Use of Land

Where at the effective date of adoption or amendment of this Ordinance, lawful use of land exists that is made no longer permissible under the terms of this ordinance as enacted or amended, such use may be continued, so long as it remains otherwise lawful, subject to the following provisions:

1. No such no-conforming use shall be enlarged or increased, nor extended to occupy a greater area of land than was occupied at the effective date of adoption or amendement of this ordinance.
1. If any such non-conforming use of land ceases for any reason for a period of more than five years, any subsequent use of such land shall conform to the regulations specified by this ordinance for the district in which such land is located.
1. In a residential zone no such non-conforming use of land shall be continued for more than five years from date of adoption of this ordinance or after effective date of annexation.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 6-1](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 6-2 Non-conforming Structure

Where a lawful structure exists at the effective date of adoption or amendment of this ordinance that could not be built under the terms of this ordinance by reasons of restrictions on area, lot coverage, height, yards or other characteristics of the structure or its location on the lot, such structure may be continued so long as it remains otherwise lawful, subject to the following provisions:

1. Such structure, upon the approval of the Board of Zoning Adjustment, may be remodeled to maintain the premises in a safe and usable condition.
1. Should such structure be destroyed by any means to an extent of more than 50 percent of its replacement cost at time of destruction, it shall not be reconstructed except in conformity with the provisions of this ordinance.
1. Such structure, upon the approval of the Board of Zoning Adjustment, may be added to if said addition meets the area requirements of the zone district in which the structure is located, provided said use of this ordinance.
1. A mobile home can be replaced by the owner on the same exact location by a substitute mobile home provided the second mobile home is 25% in fair market value more than the 1st mobile home replaced. A different location on the lot must be approved by the Planning Commission.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 6-2](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 6-3 Non-conforming Use of Structure

If a lawful use of a structure, or a structure and premises in combination, exists at the effective date of adoption or amendment of this ordinance, that would not be allowed in the district under the terms of this ordinance, the lawful use may be continued so long as it remains otherwise lawful, subject to the following provisions:

1. No existing structure devoted to a use not permitted by this ordinance in the district in which it is located shall be structurally altered except in changing the use of the structure to a use permitted in the district in which it is located.
1. Any non-conforming use may be extended to any portion of a structure arranged or designed for such non-conforming use at the time of adoption or amendment of this ordinance, but no such use shall be extended to occupy any land outside such building.
1. If no structural alterations are made, any non-conforming use of a structure, or structure and premises, may be changed to another non-conforming use provided that the Board of Adjustment shall determine that the proposed use is equally appropriate to the district as the existing non-conforming use.
1. Any non-conforming use, once changed to a conforming use, shall thereafter conform to the regulations for the district in which such structure is located and all new uses shall be conforming uses.
1. When a non-conforming use of a structure, or structure and premises in combination, is discontinued or abandoned for five consecutive years, the structure or structure and premises in combination shall not thereafter be used except in conformance with the regulations of the district in which it is located.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 6-3](/ordinances/359).*

## Section 7 — Enforcement

### Article 7-1. Enforcement Officer

1. The provisions of this Ordinance shall be administered and enforced by the Enforcement Officer designated by the City Council of Rector.
1. The Enforcement Officer shall serve as the administrative officer responsible for reviewing applications, issuing or denying permits, investigating apparent violations, issuing notices and Stop-Work Orders when authorized by this Section, and performing such other administrative duties as are assigned by this Ordinance or other duly adopted ordinances of the City.
1. Nothing in this Section shall authorize the Enforcement Officer to grant a variance from the requirements of this Ordinance or to permit a use that is not otherwise authorized by the applicable zoning district.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 1](/ordinances/599).*

### Article 7-2. Building Permit Required

1. No person, firm, corporation, property owner, contractor, or authorized agent shall commence, place, erect, construct, structurally alter, enlarge, relocate, convert, reconstruct, or install any structure or building within the incorporated limits of the City of Rector without first obtaining a Building Permit from the City when such work involves any of the following:
   1. Construction of any new principal building or principal structure;
   1. Construction of any detached accessory building or accessory structure, except as specifically exempted by Article 7-3;
   1. Any addition to an existing building or structure which increases its footprint, height, or enclosed floor area;
   1. Any structural alteration which changes the exterior dimensions, structure placement, use classification, required setbacks, or zoning compliance status of an existing structure;
   1. Relocation of any building, manufactured home, or other roofed structure onto, within, or across any lot or parcel;
   1. Installation, placement, or relocation of any manufactured home, mobile home, or prefabricated residential structure regulated by this Ordinance; or
   1. Any development activity for which zoning compliance verification is otherwise expressly required by this Ordinance.
1. A Building Permit required by this Article shall be in addition to any Certificate of Zoning Compliance, Foundation Permit, petition for placement, or other approval specifically required for a manufactured home under Article 5-7.
1. Issuance of a Building Permit shall not relieve any property owner, applicant, contractor, or other person from complying with any other applicable provision of this Ordinance or other applicable municipal, state, or federal requirement.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 1](/ordinances/599).*

### Article 7-3. Exemptions

1. A Building Permit under Article 7-2 shall not be required for the following activities unless a permit is otherwise expressly required by another City ordinance:
   1. Ordinary repairs, maintenance, or replacement in kind which do not alter the structural footprint, use, occupancy, or zoning compliance of a building or structure;
   1. Construction, repair, replacement, or enlargement of an uncovered and unenclosed residential deck, stoop, landing, or similar outdoor platform, provided that it does not support a roof or enclosure and does not create habitable or enclosed floor area;
   1. Painting, siding replacement, roofing replacement, window replacement, or interior remodeling not involving structural expansion;
   1. Portable play equipment, yard furniture, sidewalks, driveways, landscaping improvements, and similar minor site improvements;
   1. Temporary or nonpermanent movable items which do not constitute a structure as defined by this Ordinance; and
   1. A detached, prefabricated residential storage building or similar accessory structure which:
      1. Is not constructed upon or permanently attached to a poured foundation, footing, or other permanent anchoring system;
      1. Does not contain plumbing, electrical service, or facilities intended for human habitation or commercial occupancy;
      1. Is capable of being relocated without demolition or substantial reconstruction; and
      1. Is placed in compliance with the applicable setbacks, yards, easements, rights-of-way, and other requirements of this Ordinance.
1. The exemption established by subsection a(2) for decks, stoops, landings, and similar platforms shall cease to apply when a roof, walls, screening, or other enclosure is added or when the work creates or expands habitable or enclosed floor area.
1. The exemption established by subsection a(6) shall not apply to a carport, garage, dwelling, manufactured home, roofed addition attached to another structure, or any structure used for human habitation or commercial occupancy.
1. The Enforcement Officer may provide zoning-placement guidance concerning an exempt accessory structure upon request. Such guidance shall not constitute a survey, certification, or legal determination of property boundaries.
1. Exemption from the Building Permit requirement shall not exempt any person, property, building, or structure from compliance with the setbacks, yards, easements, rights-of-way, use restrictions, floodplain requirements, or other applicable provisions of this Ordinance or other applicable law.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 1](/ordinances/599).*

### Article 7-4. Application Requirements

1. Every application for a Building Permit shall be submitted to the Enforcement Officer upon forms approved by the City.
1. An application shall contain, at a minimum:
   1. The name, mailing address, and telephone number of the property owner and applicant;
   1. The street address or legal description of the property;
   1. A site sketch or plot plan showing, to the extent reasonably necessary to determine zoning compliance:
      1. Property boundaries to the extent reasonably known;
      1. The location of existing structures;
      1. The location and dimensions of the proposed construction;
      1. The proposed distances from applicable property lines, streets, alleys, and adjacent structures;
   1. A general description of the proposed work;
   1. The intended use of the building or structure;
   1. The estimated construction cost or fair market project value when necessary for calculation of the Building Permit fee; and
   1. Such additional site, dimensional, or project information as may be reasonably necessary for the Enforcement Officer to determine compliance with requirements administered by the City.
1. A professionally prepared survey shall not be required solely by reason of this Article unless reasonably necessary to resolve a material uncertainty concerning property boundaries or compliance with an applicable requirement.
1. A record of Building Permit applications, supporting site plans or sketches, permits issued or denied, and related written determinations shall be maintained by the City Clerk or within the official records of the City.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 1](/ordinances/599).*

### Article 7-5. Review and Issuance of Building Permits

1. The Enforcement Officer shall review each Building Permit application to determine whether the proposed location, dimensions, use, and general scope of work comply with:
   1. Ordinance No. 359, as amended, being the Zoning Ordinance of the City of Rector;
   1. Applicable floodplain and floodway requirements;
   1. Construction or safety codes duly adopted by the City, to the extent review or verification is reasonably within the duties and qualifications of the Enforcement Officer; and
   1. Applicable utility-connection, access, right-of-way, or municipal-service requirements established by ordinance, adopted code, easement, or written City policy.
1. The Enforcement Officer may require such reasonable information as is necessary to determine compliance with requirements administered by the City.
1. When specialized technical review, inspection, certification, or approval is required by state law or a duly adopted code and is outside the duties or qualifications of the Enforcement Officer, the applicant shall obtain the necessary review, inspection, certification, or approval from the appropriately qualified person or agency.
1. A Building Permit shall be issued when the application demonstrates compliance with the applicable requirements administered under this Article.
1. No Building Permit shall be issued when the proposed work is shown to violate an applicable requirement administered under this Article unless the applicant first obtains a variance or other relief lawfully authorized by this Ordinance.
1. Issuance of a Building Permit confirms only that the application has been approved based upon the information submitted and the municipal requirements reviewed by the City.
1. Issuance of a Building Permit shall not constitute a warranty or certification by the City concerning structural integrity, workmanship, property boundaries, or compliance with any technical code for which the City has not performed the applicable inspection or review.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 1](/ordinances/599).*

### Article 7-6. Variances and Noncomplying Applications

1. When a proposed project cannot comply with a dimensional or physical requirement of this Ordinance, including a setback, lot-area, lot-width, height, placement, or similar zoning requirement, an applicant may seek a variance from the Board of Zoning Adjustment in accordance with Section 8 of this Ordinance and Arkansas law.
1. No Building Permit shall be issued for work requiring such a variance unless and until the Board of Zoning Adjustment grants the necessary variance or other relief authorized by law.
1. Nothing in this Article shall authorize a variance permitting a use that is prohibited within the applicable zoning district.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 1](/ordinances/599).*

### Article 7-7. Stop-Work Orders

1. The Enforcement Officer may issue a written or posted Stop-Work Order when the Enforcement Officer determines that:
   1. Work requiring a Building Permit has commenced without issuance of the required permit;
   1. Construction is proceeding in material deviation from an approved Building Permit application when the deviation affects approved dimensions, placement, use, structural footprint, or zoning compliance; or
   1. Construction is proceeding in direct violation of this Ordinance or another applicable requirement administered by the Enforcement Officer, and continued work would materially affect zoning compliance, public safety, or the ability to correct the violation.
1. A Stop-Work Order shall:
   1. Identify the property;
   1. Briefly state the reason for issuance;
   1. Describe the permit, correction, approval, or other action required before work may resume; and
   1. Provide notice that a zoning determination of the Enforcement Officer may be appealed as provided by Article 7-9.
1. Upon issuance of a Stop-Work Order, all further work subject to the Order shall cease immediately except work reasonably necessary to secure the premises from danger, weather, unauthorized entry, or further damage.
1. It shall be unlawful to knowingly continue work in violation of a Stop-Work Order until authorized by the City.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 1](/ordinances/599).*

### Article 7-8. Permit Expiration and Extension

1. Every Building Permit issued pursuant to this Section shall become invalid unless the work authorized by the permit is commenced in good faith within six (6) months after the date of issuance.
1. After work has commenced, a Building Permit shall become invalid if the work authorized by the permit is suspended or abandoned for a period of six (6) consecutive months.
1. The Enforcement Officer may grant, in writing, one or more extensions of time, each for a period not exceeding six (6) months.
1. An extension shall be requested in writing, and the applicant shall demonstrate justifiable cause for the extension.
1. No fixed deadline for completion shall apply so long as the work has commenced within the applicable time period and has not thereafter been suspended or abandoned for six (6) consecutive months.
1. Expiration of a Building Permit does not legalize work performed in violation of this Ordinance and does not prohibit issuance of a new permit for work otherwise eligible for approval.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 1](/ordinances/599).*

### Article 7-9. Appeals

1. Any person aggrieved by a final determination of the Enforcement Officer concerning the enforcement or application of this Zoning Ordinance, including a zoning-based denial of a Building Permit or zoning-based Stop-Work Order, may appeal the determination to the Board of Zoning Adjustment in accordance with Section 8 of this Ordinance.
1. An appeal under this Article shall be filed in writing with the City Clerk within thirty (30) days after the person receives notice of the determination being appealed.
1. Appeals from decisions of the Board of Zoning Adjustment shall be governed by Section 8 of this Ordinance and applicable Arkansas law.
1. An appeal concerning the interpretation or enforcement of a construction, electrical, plumbing, fire, floodplain, utility, or other requirement not arising under this Zoning Ordinance shall be made according to the appeal procedure established by the applicable code or ordinance.
1. Nothing in this Article shall enlarge or diminish the jurisdiction conferred upon the Board of Zoning Adjustment by Arkansas law.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 1](/ordinances/599).*

### Article 7-10. Violations, Notice, and Penalties

1. It shall be a violation of this Ordinance for any person to:
   1. Commence or undertake work for which a Building Permit is required without first obtaining the required permit;
   1. Knowingly provide materially false or misleading information in a Building Permit application;
   1. Proceed with work in a material manner contrary to an approved Building Permit application when the deviation affects the location, dimensions, use, structural footprint, or zoning compliance of the project;
   1. Knowingly continue work in violation of a Stop-Work Order issued pursuant to this Section; or
   1. Fail to comply with a lawful requirement imposed under this Section.
1. Except when a violation creates an immediate threat to public health or safety, involves continued work in violation of a Stop-Work Order, or requires immediate action to preserve the City's legal remedies, the Enforcement Officer shall provide written notice of the alleged violation and a reasonable opportunity to:
   1. Obtain the required permit;
   1. Submit corrected or additional information;
   1. Correct the work;
   1. Seek a variance or other relief when legally available; or
   1. Otherwise bring the property into compliance.
1. Any person convicted of violating this Section shall be subject to a fine not exceeding One Hundred Dollars ($100.00).
1. When a violation is continuing in nature, each day that the violation continues after expiration of the compliance period stated in a written notice, or after issuance of an applicable Stop-Work Order requiring immediate cessation, may constitute a separate offense.
1. Payment of a fine shall not relieve any person of the obligation to obtain a required permit or correct a substantive violation.
1. In addition to any criminal penalty, the City may seek injunctive relief or any other remedy authorized by law to halt unlawful work or compel compliance.
1. Alteration or removal of construction may be sought when reasonably necessary to correct a substantive violation that cannot otherwise lawfully be resolved.
1. Failure to obtain a Building Permit, standing alone, shall not require removal of work that is otherwise capable of being approved and brought into compliance with all applicable requirements.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 1](/ordinances/599).*

### Article 8-4 Variance

1. To authorize upon appeal in specific cases such variance from the terms
    of this ordinance as will not be contrary to the public interest where,
    owing to special conditions, a literal enforcement of the provisions of
    this ordinance would result in unnecessary hardship. A variance from the
    terms of this ordinance may be granted by the Board of Zoning Adjustment
    in the following instances only:

    1. A written application for a variance is submitted demonstrating:

        1. The property subject to the variance possesses unique conditions not generally applicable to other properties in the same district, or is subject to physical, topographical, or geologic limitations that restrict its use.
        1. The grant of the variance will not grant any special privilege to the property owner or the owner's authorized agent.
        1. The applicant can demonstrate that without a variance there can be no reasonable use of the property.
        1. The grant of the variance is not based solely on economic reasons.
        1. The necessity for the variance was not created by the property owner or the owner's authorized agent.
        1. The grant of the variance will not be injurious to the public health, safety, or welfare.
1. In granting any variance, the Board of Adjustment may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards to insure the protection of the adjacent property owners.
1. Under no circumstances shall the Board of Adjustment grant a variance to permit a use not generally or by special exception permitted in the district involved.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 591, § 3](/ordinances/591); Article 8-4(B)–(C) retained from [Ordinance 359](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 8-5 Appeals from Decisions of the Board

1. Appeal from the decision of the board of adjustment shall be the city council within 30 days from the decision of the board.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 591, § 4](/ordinances/591).*

### Article 8-6 Notices and Fees

1. Whenever an appeal or application for a variance is made to the Board, the Board shall cause to have published at the expense of the appellant or applicant a notice of the time and place of the public hearing upon such appeal or application, which notice shall be published at least once not less than seven (7) days preceding the date of such hearing in a paper of general circulation in Piggott, said notice to designate the particular location with which the appeal or application is concerned, and a brief statement as to what the appeal or application consists of. The Board shall also give or cause to be given such additional notice of such hearing to interested persons and organizations as it shall deem feasible and practicable.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 8-6](/ordinances/359).*

## Section 9 — Amendments

### Article 9-1 Amendment to Text

1. The City Council may suggest that the Planning Commission amend the text of this ordinance or the Planning Commission itself may desire to initiate an amendment. Should the Planning Commission, after study, request a change in the text, it shall conduct a public hearing on the proposed amendment. Following the public hearing, such recommendations shall be submitted to the City Council for adoption.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 9-1](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 9-2 Change in Classification

1. A petition giving the legal description of the property involved and the zoning classification requested for the property shall be submitted to the Planning Commission by the property owner or his legally designated agent. The petition shall also include a statement and diagram explaining why the proposed changes will not conflict with surrounding land uses.
1. Upon receipt of the petition for an amendment, the Planning Commission, in accordance with Act 186 of the 1957 General Assembly as subsequently amended, shall proceed as follows:
   1. The Planning Commission shall hold a public hearing on a proposed amendment. Notice of the public hearing shall be published in a newspaper of general circulation in the City, at least one time fifteen (15) days prior to the hearing.
   1. Following the public hearing, the proposed amendment may be approved as presented of in modified form by a majority vote of the Planning Commission and recommended for adoption by the City Council, with reasons for such recommendation stated in writing.
   1. If the Planning Commission disapproves a proposed amendment, the reasons for such disapproval shall be given in writing to the petitioner within thirty (30) days from the date of the hearing.
   1. The City Council, by majority vote, may be ordinance adopt the recommended amendment submitted by the Planning Commission for further study and recommendation.

      If the City Council does not concur with the recommendation of the Planning Commission, either as first submitted or as submitted after re-study the City Council may, by a majority vote, amend this ordinance by granting the request for amendment in full or in modified form.
   1. Following disapproval of a proposed amendment by the Planning Commission, the petitioner may appeal such disapproval to the City Council, provided that the petitioner states specifically in writing to the City Clerk why he considers the Planning Commission's findings and decisions are in error. Such appeal shall be filed with the City Clerk within fifteen (15) days following disapproval by the Planning Commission.
1. No application for a zoning amendment will be considered by the Planning Commission within twelve (12) months from date of final disapproval of a proposed amendment unless the Commission finds that a substantial reason exists for waiving this limitation.
1. Before any action shall be taken as provided in this section, any person or persons proposing a change in the zoning regulations or district boundaries shall deposit with the City Clerk the sum of twenty-five dollars ($25.00) to cover the approximate cost of this procedure, and under no condition shall said sum or any part thereof be refunded for failure of said change to be adopted by the City Council.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 9-2](/ordinances/359).*

## Section 10 — Severability and emergency

### Article 10-1 Severability

1. The provisions of this ordinance are hereby declared to be severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, or clause of this ordinance shall be held invalid, the invalidity of such section, paragraph, sentence, or clause shall not affect the validity of the remainder of the said ordinance.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 10-1](/ordinances/359).*

### Article 10-2 Emergency

1. It being ascertained and found that the passage of this ordinance will promote in accordance with present and future needs, the safety, order, convenience, efficienty and economy in the process of development; convenience in population distribution, good civic design and arrangement; and adequate provisions for public utilities and other public improvements; the passage therefore is found necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health, safety, and general welfare, and an emergency thus existing, this ordinance shall take effect upon its passage and publication.
{: .upper-alpha}

*Source: [Ordinance 359, incorporated regulations, Article 10-2](/ordinances/359).*

## Related permit fees and state-law provision

### Building permit fees

A Building Permit shall be required whenever required by Section 7 of Ordinance No. 359, as amended by this Ordinance, regardless of whether a fee is charged.

The following fee schedule shall apply:

1. For proposed work having an estimated project value of Two Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($2,500.00) or less, no Building Permit fee shall be charged.
1. For proposed work having an estimated project value greater than Two Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($2,500.00), but not exceeding Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000.00), the Building Permit fee shall be Fifty Dollars ($50.00).
1. For proposed work having an estimated project value greater than Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000.00), the Building Permit fee shall be Fifty Dollars ($50.00), plus Two Dollars ($2.00) for each additional One Thousand Dollars ($1,000.00), or fraction thereof, by which the project value exceeds Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000.00).
1. In no event shall the Building Permit fee imposed under this Section exceed Two Thousand Dollars ($2,000.00) for a single permit.
1. Project value shall include the reasonable value of labor and materials necessary to complete the work subject to the permit.

*Source: [Ordinance 599, § 2](/ordinances/599).*

### Consistency with state law

Nothing in this Ordinance shall be interpreted or administered in a manner inconsistent with Arkansas Code Annotated § 14-56-205 concerning accessory dwelling units or other applicable state law.

*Source: [Ordinance 601, § 3](/ordinances/601).*
