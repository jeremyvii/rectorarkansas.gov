**Entitled: An Ordinance to regulate the use of water from water mains, hydrants and connections with water mains of the Water Improvement District No. 1 of the City of Rector, and prescribing penalties for the violation thereof.**

Be it Ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector.

## Section 1.

It shall be unlawful for any person, company or corporation to use water from any hydrant or water main of the Water Improvement District No. 1 of the City of Rector, without having first paid the charges fixed by the Commissioners of said Water Improvement District No. 1 of the City of Rector.

## Section 2.

No person, company or corporation shall install or put on water pipes upon their property without also installing a separate cut-off for each line delivering water to each subscriber, and every cut-off shall be located at the curb opposite the place of delivery or in the border of said property and shall at all times be kept subject to the use of the agents of said Water Improvement District No. 1 of the City of Rector, and said agents shall be allowed to enter upon the premises to examine any and all fixtures of subscribers upon the application to the owners.

## Section 3.

Any subscriber allowing hydrants or water openings, of any kind, to drip or waste water, when not in actual use, may be disconnected from said water main at a cur-off or otherwise if same becomes necessary to stop the violation of this provision of this ordinance, and any amount paid by said subscriber shall be forfeited in addition to the other penalties herein prescribed.

## Section 4.

Any person, firm or corporation violating any of the provisions of Section One, Two or Three, of this Ordinance, shall upon conviction thereof, be fined in any sum not exceeding the sum of One Hundred Dollars.

## Section 5.

That this ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage.

Passed and approved this the 2nd day of February, 1925.

/s D. G. Moseley  
Mayor

/s R. E. French  
Recorder
