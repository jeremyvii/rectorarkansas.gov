**An ordinance modifying city water and sewage reconnect fees for the City of Rector, Arkansas**

Whereas, the City of Rector incurs great costs in operating the water and sewer in the City of Rector and has not issued an increase in several years and is therefore losing money and unable to operate to serve citizens of Rector; and

Whereas, it has been determined by the Rector City Council that the reconnect fee shall be increased to $40.00, and

Whereas, it has been determined that the time period for the disconnect policy should be revised.

Now therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows:

## Section 1.

It has been determined that effective November 1, 2010, the water and sewage department of the City of Rector will mail all water and sewage bills on the 1st business day of every month with all bills due on the 10th of the month, or if the 10th falls on a non business day, the next available business day. Any bills that remain outstanding on the 10th of the month will result in late fees. If not paid by the 25th of said month it will result in disconnection of service.

## Section 2.

It has been determined that effective November 1, 2010, the water and sewage department of the City of Rector should increase the reconnect fee to $40.00

## Section 3.

It is determined by the City of Rector due to the need for matters to be resolved in the coming months, and an emergency is declared to exist and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its passage

## Section 4.

All Ordinances and parts of Ordinances in conflict within the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. Unless specifically amended herein, all other portions of previous water and sewage Ordinances shall remain in full force and effect.

Passed and approved on this 14th day of September, 2010.

Mayor Ron Kemp

Attest: City Clerk, Lowell McKinney
