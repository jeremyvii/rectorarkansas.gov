**AN ORDINANCE TRANSFERRING ADMINISTRATION OF RETIREMENT COVERAGE FOR ELIGIBLE MEMBERS OF THE RECTOR FIRE PENSION AND RELIEF FUND TO THE ARKANSAS LOCAL POLICE AND FIRE RETIREMENT SYSTEM (LOPFI) AND AUTHORIZING THE CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER TO ENTER INTO AN IRREVOCABLE AGREEMENT WITH LOPFI.**

WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees of the RECTOR Fire Pension and Relief Fund (Local Plan) has elected to have its Local Plan administered by LOPFI as stated by Resolution dated 8-1-17, 2017, and that

WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees has requested by Resolution that the City of RECTOR, Arkansas to act as its agent henceforth.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS:

## SECTION 1.

If accepted by LOPFI, the administration of the RECTOR Fire Pension and Relief Fund (Local Plan) shall be transferred to LOPFI under authority of Arkansas Code Annotated 24-10-101, et. seq., as amended. Such transfer shall mean the administration of the Local Plan only and not a change in the Local Plan’s benefit program unless a benefit increase is approved by the City of RECTOR, Arkansas.

## SECTION 2.

The Chief Administrative Officer is hereby authorized to enter into an irrevocable agreement, *using a **fifteen (15)** or twenty-five (25) year amortization schedule (drafter of Ordinance needs to select one amortization period)*, with LOPFI to administer the Local Plan as stated in Section 1 hereof.

## SECTION 3.

It is the intent of the City Council and it is hereby ordained that the provisions of this Ordinance shall be codified into the Code of Ordinances of RECTOR, Arkansas and the sections thereof may be re-numbered and re-lettered as necessary to accomplish such intention.

## SECTION 4.

Due to the need to consolidate administration of the Local Plan with LOPFI, an emergency is hereby declared to exist and this Ordinance shall be in full force from and after the date of its passage and approval.
