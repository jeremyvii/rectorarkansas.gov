**An ordinance authorizing the City of Rector, Arkansas, to transact and conduct business with Comfort Control Cooling and for other purposes.**

Whereas, it is necessary for the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, to transact or conduct business from time to time with a heating and air company which is located in the city of Rector; and

Whereas, Comfort Control Cooling offers said services to the citizens of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

Therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows:

## Section 1.:

It is determined by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas to acquire the above described services with Comfort Control Cooling.

Passed and approved on this 5th day of March, 2007.

Ronald E. Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Lowell D. McKenney, City Clerk
