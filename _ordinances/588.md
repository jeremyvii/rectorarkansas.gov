**An ordinance accepting the annexation of certain territory to the City of Rector of Arkansas; approving the schedule of services to be extended to said area; and assigning such to ward, and for other matters**

Whereas, a petition was filed, pursuant to Ark. Code Annotated Section 14-40-601, et. seq. for the annexation of certain territory into the City of Rector, Arkansas; and

Whereas, the majority of the landowners have prepared a complete and accurate petition as set forth in Ark. Code Ann. Section 14-40-601, et. seq.; and

Whereas, the property is contiguous with the City of Rector; verified that no enclaves will be created if the Petition is accepted by the City, and presented the petition to their respective Clay County Judge; and

Whereas, the County Judge held a county court proceeding on July 2, 2025, has (A) reviewed the Petition and verifications for completeness and accuracy; (B) determined that no enclaves will be created by the annexation; (C) confirmed that the Petition contains a schedule of services; (D) found that the land to be annexed is in accordance with the Statute and (E) issued an Order articulating these findings and forwarded the Petition to the City of Rector for considerations; and

Whereas, it is the desire of the City of Rector Council that said territory be annexed.

Now therefore be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, State of Arkansas

## Section 1.

That the following described territory, contiguous to the City of Rector, be and the same is hereby accepted as part of, and annexed to and made a part of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

See Exhibit A.

## Section 2.

That the tract of land does not require any additional water or sewer services to be added and that police and fire will begin immediately upon approval of annexation.

## Section 3.

That the above-described territory shall be annexed to and make a part of each contiguous ward of the City of Rector, and the same shall henceforth be a part of said ward as fully as existing parts of said ward. If said property abuts a portion of more than one ward, the portion that abuts a majority of either possible ward shall be the ward that is designated the annexed parcel.

## Section 4.

Thirty (30) days after passage and publication or posting of this Ordinance as authorized by law, the annexation shall be final and the property shall be within the corporate limits of the City of Rector, except as otherwise ordered by the Circuit Court pursuant to a cause of action filed within said thirty (30) day period.

## Section 5.

Notice. Within forty-five (45) days of the effective date of this ordinance the city clerk shall provide written notice, along with complete documentation, to the county clerk of each county in which a territory is affected.

Passed and approved this 11th September day of 2025.

Debi Boyd, City Clerk

[illegible], Mayor of Rector
