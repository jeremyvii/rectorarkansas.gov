**An ordinance to amend the zoning ordinance of the City of Rector, Arkansas, to require a minimum of five contiguous acres excluding all buildings or other structures before property will be considered for agricultural zoning.**

Whereas: It has come to the attention of the Rector City council that it would be in the best interest of the citizens of Rector that the Zoning Ordinance be revised.

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the Rector City Council as follows, to-wit:

That Ordinance No. 359, Article 5-1, Section B, is hereby amended to read as follows:

### B. Area Requirements

1. Each parcel of land must consist of at least five contiguous acres, excluding all buildings or other structures.

   **Height Regulations.**

1. The maximum height of a structure shall be two stories and not to exceed twenty-four (24) feet.

### B.

The Planning Commission may waive the height requirements when it is demonstrated that equipment and the structure to house an agricultural operation require greater height.

Whereas: It has been determined that this ordinance is necessary and vital to the health, welfare and safety of the public, therefore, an emergency is hereby declared to exist and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage and approval.

Ronald E. Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Kenneth R. Burns, City Treasurer/Recorder

Dated: 3/3/03
