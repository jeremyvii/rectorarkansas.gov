**AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RATES ON MOSQUITO REDUCTION FEE PROGRAM, ORDINANCE 540, IN ORDER TO FAIRLY INSTITUTE RATES WITH MULTI UNIT RENTALS PROPERTIES; AND PRESCRIBING OTHER MATTERS PERTAINING THERETO;**

WHEREAS, a Mosquito Reduction Fee Program was passed by the voters of the City of Rector;

WHEREAS, the City of Rector seeks to modify, as allowed, the rates for Multi Unit Rental Property.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas and the following section shall be added:

## Section 10.

The Mosquito Reduction Fee shall be modified as follows for multi unit rental property within the City of Rector.

| Units | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| 1-5 units | $3.00/month |
| 6-15 units | $2.75/month |
| 16-30 units | $2.50/month |
| 31 or more | $2.00/month |

WHEREAS; All Ordinances and parts of Ordinances in conflict within the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. Unless specifically amended herein, all other portions of Ordinance No. 540 shall remain in full force and effect.

PASSED AND APPROVED on this 7th day of March, 2011.

Mayor David Freeman

ATTEST: City Clerk, Lowell McKinney
