**AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS, TO ALLOW HEATH MCCLUNG AND TERESA ROOFE TO CONTINUE WITH PREVIOUS DUTIES PRIOR TO BEING EMPLOYED WITH THE CITY OF RECTOR.**

WHEREAS, it is necessary for the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, to allow city employees Heath McClung and Teresa Roofe to continue providing services to the City of Rector in other capacities; and

WHEREAS, Heath McClung washed city vehicles prior to his employment as a policeman with the City of Rector and Teresa Roofe served as the treasurer of the Woodland Heights Cemetery Commission prior to her term as a member of the city council; and

WHEREAS, both employees were performing the services prior to their employment with the City of Rector and both provide separate services outside of their employment with the City of Rector.

THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS, AS FOLLOWS:

## SECTION 1.:

It is determined by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas to allow the above described services to coexist with both employees.

PASSED AND APPROVED on this 21st day of September, 2009.

Mayor Ronald E. Kemp

ATTEST: Lowell D. McKenney, City Clerk
