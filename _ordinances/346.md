**An ordinance providing for the acceptance of dedication of North Phillips Street and Stewart Street by the Housing Authority of the City of Rector, Arkansas.**

Be it ordained and enacted by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, to-wit:

1. That a dedication of North Phillip Street and Stewart Street by the Housing Authority of the City of Rector, Arkansas, on July 31, 1974, be accepted this date, with the provisions and restrictions as established in the dedication.
1. This ordinance shall take effect and be in full force from and after its passage and approval by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas.
1. This ordinance being necessary for the preservation of public health, peace, prosperity, safety, order, comfort, enjoyment and convenience of the City of Rector, Araknsas and the inhabitants thereof and an emergency is hereby declared to exist and the members of the City Council hereby unanimously and expressly dispense with the rule required by Section 19-2404, Arkansas Statutes Annotated requiring the reading of an ordinance on three different days before passage thereof.
{: .ordinance-list-decimal}

This ordinance adopted, passed and approved this 5th day of February, 1979.

Bill Vanlandingham, Mayor

Attest: Donna Reed
