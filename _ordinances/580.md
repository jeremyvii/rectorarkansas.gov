**An ordinance amending Ordinance No. 310 of the City Housing Authority, and for other purposes**

Whereas, the City Council has reviewed existing terms and appointments of all commissions; and

Whereas, the City Council desires to have continuity to long term commission members and their institutional knowledge within each commission; and

Whereas, in an effort to allow new appointments and successor appointment to remain, commission succession shall be revised for clarity; and

Now therefore be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector Arkansas:

That the following shall be revised to Ordinance 310:

## Section 1.

The Fourth (4th) Whereas clause shall add the following language.

All current members of the commission are eligible for reappointment and nothing within this Ordinance or it’s amendments shall cause to prevent current members from being reappointed.

## Section 2.

All other parts of the ordinance shall remain in effect. No other provisions of the ordinance shall be amended or modified.

## Section 3.

If any provision of this amendment is held to be invalid, only those portions that are held to be invalid can be severed.

Dated this 4th day of March, 2024.

City of Rector, Arkansas

By: Shawn Brandon, Mayor

Attest: Debi Boyd, City Clerk
