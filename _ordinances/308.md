**AN ORDINANCE ENACTING THE CREATION OF A FIVE-MAN PARK AND RECREATION COMMISSION FOR THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS.**

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS, UNDER AUTHORITY OF ARKANSAS STATUTE 19-3601 - 19-3645 (INCLUSIVE):

## SECTION I.

That on and after the passage and approval of this ordinance, a Five-Man Park and Recreation Commission will be created for the purpose of operating the parks and recreational facilities for the City of Rector.

## SECTION II.

Said Commission shall be appointed by the Mayor and confirmed by a majority vote of the duly elected and qualified members of the City Council and shall hold office for a term of five (5) years, provided, however, that those commissioners first appointed and confirmed after the passage of this ordinance shall serve for terms of one (1), two (2), three (3), four (4), and five (5) years each, to be designated by the Mayor and City Council, and therefore, upon the expiration of their respective terms, commissioners appointed by the Mayor and approved by the majority vote of the City Council shall each be appointed for a term of five (5) years. Each commissioner shall be a qualified elector of the city.

## SECTION III.

Any commissioner appointed by the provision of this statute may be removed for cause upon a two-thirds (2/3) vote of the duly elected, qualified members of the City Council.

## SECTION IV.

The commission shall adopt such rules and regulations as they may deem necessary and expedient for the proper operation and management of such municipal parks and recreation programs and shall have authority to alter, change and amend such rules and regulations at their discretion.

## SECTION V.

The commissioners appointed shall have full and complete authority to build, manage and operate, maintain and keep in a good state of repair any building deemed necessary to carry on a park and recreation program for said municipality.

Said commission shall have full and complete charge of said buildings and grounds, including the right to control and permit or refuse to permit such public gatherings or other meetings or affairs as the commission shall see fit and deem to be to the best interest of the city.

## SECTION VI.

The City Council may at any time as it deems necessary appropriate money from the General Revenue Fund or such other funds that the city may have available for the purpose of maintaining and operating the said recreational park.

## SECTION VII.

All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed and this ordinance being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health and safety, an emergency is hereby declared to exist, and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage and approved.

Passed and approved by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, this 7 day of September, 1971.

H. R. French  
MAYOR
