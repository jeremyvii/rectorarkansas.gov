**An ordinance to regulate the movement of traffic by the placement of 4-way stop signs and to regulate speed on Woodland Heights Drive within the City of Rector and for other purposes.**

Whereas: The excessive speed on Woodland Heights Drive is creating a dangerous condition for the citizens of Rector.

Whereas: The City Council of Rector, considers it in the best interest of the citizens of Rector to place 4-way stop signs at certain intersections on Woodland Heights Drive and to restrict the speed within the area.

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the Council of Rector, Arkansas:

## Section 1:

From and after passage of this ordinance, there shall be a 4-way stop sign placed at the intersection of Woodland Heights Drive and Allen Drive.

## Section 2:

From and after passage of this ordinance there shall be placed a 4-way stop sign at the intersection of Woodland Heights Drive and 2nd Street.

## Section 3:

The speed limit shall be 30 MPH from the City limits (Cemetery) to West 5th Street. Stop sign shall be placed at a minimum of 4 each side of Woodland Heights to indicate the speed limit to the motorists.

## Section 4:

Any driver of any vehicle who violates any provision of this ordinance shall be guilty of a moving violation and upon conviction before the Court of Competent jurisdiction shall be guilty of a Misdemeanor and shall be fined the sum of not less than $25.00 nor more than $100.00 plus court cost.

## Section 5:

All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed.

## Section 6:

This ordinance being necessary for the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Rector and necessary for effective meeting, there being a significant scheduling problem for members of the City Council within the City of Rector, an emergency is declared to exist and this ordinance shall take effect and being in force and after its passage and publication.

Passed and approved this 2nd day of February, 1998.

By: Mayor Ronald E. Kemp

Attest: Recorder/Treasurer Kenneth R. Burns
