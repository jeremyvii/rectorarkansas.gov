**An ordinance allowing mayor Ron Kemp to borrow an amount not to exceed $50,000.00 for the construction of a new Little League field for the City of Rector.**

Whereas, the City of Rector needs funding for a new Little League Field; and

Whereas, it will cost approximately $50,000.00 to complete construction of said Little League Field; and

Whereas, Arkansas law allows the City of Rector to authorize the Mayor to enter into an agreement to obligate the City in borrowing funds to complete this kind of project as long as the note does not extend longer than a period of five (5) years; and

Whereas, the Rector City Council has voted to allow the Mayor to seek the funding with local area banks in order to secure the best interest rate available; and

Whereas, an emergency exists in which this Ordinance should take effect immediately so that the Little League Field will be ready for next season and therefore generate revenue to pay back the loan.

Now therefore be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas:

## Section 1:

That Mayor Ron Kemp is hereby authorized to borrow up to $50,000.00 on behalf of the City of Rector with a local area bank, with the best interest rate, for a lending period not to exceed five (5) years and said funds shall be used to construct a new Little League Field.

## Section 2:

That an emergency is hereby declared to exist in order to provide the City of Rector with a Little League Field, by the next season, so that said Little League Field can generate the revenue to pay back the above mentioned loan, and thus provide for the health, safety and welfare of the community.

Passed this 4th day of October, 2004.

Attest: Kenny Burns, city clerk

Ron Kemp, mayor
