Whereas, the City Council for the City of Rector, being in a regular session this 18th day of December 2002 and;

Whereas, the Council, having been duly advised that the City should, pursuant to Arkansas Law, adopt an amended budget for 2002, and;

Whereas, the City Council has been presented a 2002 amended budget for the General Fund, Street Fund, and other funds of the City of Rector, and that a true copy of the 2002 Amended Budget as affixed hereto and marked Exhibit "A" and made part of this Ordinance as though set forth word for word herein, and;

Whereas, the Council carefully examined the 2002 Amended Budget and after motion having been duly made and seconded and voted on by the Rector City Council, the 2002 Amended Budget is hereby declared to be the subject of the following findings:

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the governmental body the City of Rector, Arkansas, that the 2002 Amended Budget, which is in words and terms as set forth in the attached Exhibit "A" for the General Fund, Street Fund, and other funds of the City of Rector is hereby adopted this 19th day of December, 2002.

## Emergency clause

Because of immediate need to provide definition and permitted use of the 2002 Amended Budget, an emergency is declared to exist and the Ordinance shall be in force and effect as of the date of its passage and first publication.

Passed and approved this 18th day of December, 2002.

City of Rector, Arkansas

Ronald E. Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Kenneth R. Burns, Recorder & Treasurer
