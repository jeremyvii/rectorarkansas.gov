**An ordinance amending Ordinance No. 307 and 453 of the City of Rector Arkansas regulating the keeping of dogs and applying breeders license within the city, and for other matters pertaining thereto**

Whereas, the City Council has seen a developing need for monitoring of dog ownership and excessive animal ownership that threatens the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens and the dogs; and

Whereas, the City Council understands this need and appreciates the varying degrees stages of dog ownership, as is allowed in similarly situated other towns in the State of Arkansas; and

Now therefore be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector Arkansas:

## Section 1.

That the following shall be revised to Ordinance 307 and added to the end of Section 1.

It shall be unlawful for any person, household, or residence to own, keep, or harbor more than five (5) dogs, which are more than 16 weeks old within the corporate city limits.

The burden of proof to reflect the age shall be on the owners to show verification of the age. Keeping of more than five (5) dogs will be prima facie evidence of the violation and the burden shall be on the owners.

It shall be unlawful for any person to engage in the breeding of dogs without a breeder’s license on file with the City Clerk. Each breeder will be required to meet with standards of reasonable care and shall be subject to inspection by a member of law enforcement or the city inspector’s office. The breeders shall be operated in connection with minimum standards imposed by State and Federal Guidelines for sheltering operations. A member of law enforcement or the city inspector’s office shall have the ability to inspect the harboring of such facility to structure that is housing more than five (5) dogs upon a reasonable cause of determining more than five (5) dogs are in existence.

## Section 2.

That the following shall be amended in Ordinance 453 and Section 1 and 2 shall be modified as follows:

That any owners that is in violation of this ordinance and allows their dog to run at loose shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction shall be subject to a fine not less than $100.00 nor more than $200.00 and each day that said dog is allowed to run at large shall be considered a separate offense and punishable as such.

If any owner is in violation of owning dogs in violation of this ordinance, shall be imposed a fine of $100.00 and nor more than $200.00 and each day they are in violation of this ordinance shall be considered a separate offense and punishable as such.

## Section 3:

All other parts of the ordinance shall remain in effect. No other provisions of the ordinance shall be amended or modified.

Dated this 10th day of September, 2026.

City of Rector, Arkansas
