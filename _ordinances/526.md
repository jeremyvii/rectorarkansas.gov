**AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 379 PASSED AND APPROVED AUGUST 17TH, 1987, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES**

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS:

That Ordinance No. 379 is hereby amended as follows:

## SECTION 4:

Any violation of this ordinance shall be deemed a misdemeanor and upon conviction shall be punishable by a fine. The first violation of any provision of this Ordinance shall be punishable by a fine of $100.00; a second violation within 12 months shall be punishable by a fine of $200.00; and the third violation within 12 months shall be punishable by a fine of $500.00. Further, each violation is respective only to the person and not to individual properties, i.e., the violation will run with the person and not to the respective property only.

All other portions of Ordinance No. 379 shall remain in full force and effect.

WHEREAS; It is determined by the City of Rector due to the need for matters to be resolved before the next council meeting. therefore that an emergency is declared to exist and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its passage.

WHEREAS; All Ordinances and parts of Ordinances in conflict within the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. Unless specifically amended herein, all other portions of Ordinance No. 379 shall remain in full force and effect.

PASSED AND APPROVED on this 1st day of October, 2007.

Mayor Ron Kemp

ATTEST: City Clerk, Lowell McKinney
