**An Ordinance, "Entitled", an Ordinance to vacate all that part of Dooley Street and Ballard Avenue lying within the boundaries of the Northeast fourth, of the Northwest fourth, of the Northeast fourth, of Section (26), Twenty-six, Township (19) North, Range (7) East, in the Eastern District of Clay County, Arkansas.**

Be it Ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

## Section 1.

That all that part of Dooley Street and of Ballard Avenue lying within the boundaries of the Northeast fourth, of the Northwest fourth of the Northeast Fourth, of Section (26) Twenty-six, Township (19) Nineteen North, Range (7) East, be, and the same is hereby vacated and the owners of the lands abutting thereon are hereby authorized to take charge of the same

## Section 2.

All Ordinances and parts of Ordinances in conflict with this ordinance is hereby repealed and this ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage.

Approved this the 9th day of June 1925.

/s D. G. Moseley  
Mayor

/s R. H. Wall  
Recorder Pro Tem.
