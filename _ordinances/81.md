**Entitled an ordinance to Abate Nuisance.**

Be it ordained by the Town Council of the Incorporated Town of Rector.

## Sec. 1.

It shall hereafter be unlawful for any person or persons, corporation, business firm or firms, to sweep or throw upon the sidewalks or streets of the Incorporated Town of Rector, any paper, rubbish, dirt, trash or waste matter of any kind whatever with or without a name that would produce a nuisance.

## Sec. 2.

All persons, corporations or business firms shall hereafter sweep or throw all papers, rubbish, dirt, trash and refuse into the waste baskets, left upon the sidewalks and streets for that purpose, to be carried away by the scavenger or City Marshall and burned.

## Sec. 3.

Any person, or persons, corporation or business firms who shall violate any of the provisions of Section (1) One and (2) Two of this ordinance shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be fined in any sum not less than One Dollar nor more than Twenty-five Dollars.

## Sec. 4.

That this ordinance shall take effect and be in full force from and after its passage.

Approved: September 2, 1912

Signed: Porter Vance,  
Recorder

Signed: M. Outlaw,  
Mayor
