**"An Ordinance regulating the riding of bicycles."**

"Be it ordained by the Council of the Incorporated Town of Rector."

## Sec. 1.

It shall hereafter be unlawful for any person riding a bicycle on any sidewalk in Rector to pass or attempt to pass any other person while walking or standing on the same sidewalk.

## Sec. 2.

Any person found guilty of violating this ordinance shall be fined in any sum not less than One Dollar nor more than Twenty-five ($25.00) dollars.

## Sec. 3.

This ordinance shall take effect on and after its passage.

Approved: June 3, 1901.

Signed: Hardy Neely,  
Recorder

Signed: A. West  
Mayor
