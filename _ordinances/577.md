**AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND 548 AND ORDINANCE 563 ADJUSTING TO CONSISTENT SEWER SERVICES RENDERED THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS; AND PRESCRIBING OTHER MATTERS RELATING THERETO.**

WHEREAS, The City of Rector, Arkansas (the “City”) owns and operates water and sewer facilities, which water and sewer facilities are operated as one interrelated municipal undertaking (the “System”); and

WHEREAS, it is necessary for the City to increase the rates to be charged for the services of the System in order to finance improvements to the System, which are necessary to make the services thereof adequate for the needs of the city;

WHEREAS, the City has been under a consent decree and in order to assure safe and habitable water for the citizens, the rising costs of infrastructure, and to provide a consistent basis for expenses for the sewer and water billing, the City Council must increase rates to continue to provide such services to its citizens.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas:

## Section 1.

That Section 2 of Ordinance 548 and 563 shall be amended as follow:

The following monthly rates and charges which the City Council hereby finds and declares are fair, reasonable and minimum rates to be charged for the sewer services be, and they are hereby, fixed as rates to be rendered by the system.

Monthly Sewer Rates. All sewer charges shall be based on water consumption and the amount to be paid by each customer shall be computed on the basis of the following schedule of rates:
