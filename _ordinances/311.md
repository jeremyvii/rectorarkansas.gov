**An ordinance creating a board of five (5) trustees for the purpose of operating and maintaining the Rector Public Library.**

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas:

## Section 1.

The Mayor of the City of Rector shall appoint a Board of five (5) Trustees for the purpose of operating and maintaining the Rector Public Library. Said trustees shall receive no compensation for their services and shall make an oath of affirmation before entering office.

## Section 2.

The term of office shall be as follows: two trustees shall hold office for two (2) years; two trustees shall hold office for four (4) years and one trustee shall hold office for six years from the 1st day of January following their appointment. Each trustee appointed thereafter shall hold office for six (6) years. Vacancies on the board shall be filled by the Mayor with the approval of the City Council. If no successor is appointed by the Mayor, said trustees shall continue to hold office until a replacement is appointed.

## Section 3.

Said trustees shall immediately after their appointment, meet and organize and duly elect one of their number as president and one as secretary-treasurer and the election of such other officers as they may deem necessary. They shall make and adopt such by-laws, rules and regulations as they deem proper.

## Section 4.

All monies received for library purposes shall belong to and be designated as the Rector Library Fund and shall be paid to the Secretary-Treasurer of the Board and kept separate and apart from other funds of the City of Rector. Said funds shall be disbursed by the proper officers of the library trustees. Said board shall have exclusive control of the expenditures of all monies collected for the benefit of the library fund; the Board of Trustees shall supervise the construction of any library building, and the control, care and custody of the grounds, rooms or buildings constructed, leased or set apart for a library purpose.

## Section 5.

Said Board shall at the end of the calendar year make a written report to the City Council. Said report shall be verified by the President and Secretary and shall contain the following information:

<ol type="a" style="list-style-type: none;">
  <li>(a) an itemized statement of the various sums of money received for the library fund, by contribution and gifts;</li>
  <li>(b) a statement of the real and personal property received by devise, bequest, purchase, gift or otherwise.</li>
</ol>

## Section 6.

Any trustee may be removed from office for cause or without cause, by a two-thirds majority vote of the members of the city council.

## Section 7.

All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed and this ordinance shall be in force and effect from and after its passage and approval.

Passed and approved by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, this 7 day of August, 1972.

H. R. French, Mayor

Attest:

Lavern Manchester, Recorder
