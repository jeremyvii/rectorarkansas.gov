**An ordinance fixing rates for water and sewer services rendered by the water and sewer system of the City of Rector, Arkansas; amending ordinance 548, 563, 577, 589 and prescribing other matters relating thereto.**

Whereas, The City of Rector, Arkansas (the “City”) owns and operates water and sewer facilities, which water and sewer facilities are operated as one interrelated municipal undertaking (the “System”); and

Whereas, it is necessary for the City to increase the rates to be charged for the services of the System in order to finance improvements to the System, which are necessary to make the services thereof adequate for the needs of the city;

Now, therefore be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas:

## Section 1.

That Section 2 of Ordinance 589 establishing sewer rates shall be amended as follows:

Monthly Sewer Rates. <mark>All meters will be charged the minimum fees.</mark> All sewer charges shall be based on water consumption and the amount to be paid by each customer shall be computed on the basis of the following schedule of rates:

### Residential sewer customers (inside and outside city limits)

<table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Water consumption</th><th scope="col">Effective</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><th scope="row">For the first 2,000 gallons of water consumption per month, or portion thereof</th><td>$19.21 (minimum)</td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row">For the next 4,000 gallons of water consumption per month, or portion thereof</th><td>6.89 per 1,000 gallons</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

### Commercial sewer customers

<table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Water consumption</th><th scope="col">Effective</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><th scope="row">For the first 2,000 gallons of water consumption per month, or portion thereof</th><td>$25.06 (minimum)</td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row">For the next 4,000 gallons of water consumption per month, or portion thereof</th><td>6.89 per 1,000 gallons</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

## Section 2.

<mark>That Section 1 of Ordinance 589 shall be amended to impose a minimum water charge of $15.37 plus applicable city sales tax for any zero reading meter that is on a customer’s water account.</mark>

## Section 3.

That the provisions of this Ordinance are separable and if a section, phrase or provision shall be declared invalid, such declaration shall not affect the validity of the remainder of the Ordinance.

## Section 4.

That all ordinances and resolutions and parts thereof in conflict herewith are hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.
