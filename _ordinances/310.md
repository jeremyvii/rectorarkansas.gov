**Resolution no. 310**

**A resolution creating a "Housing Authority" within the City of Rector, Arkansas.**

Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas as follows, to-wit:

Whereas, a previous resolution was passed by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, creating a "Housing Authority", and

Whereas, it has been determined that said resolution has been misplaced or destroyed; and

Whereas, the City of Rector, in compliance with Arkansas Statute 19-3004 reaffirms the previous resolution for the following reasons and needs of the city:

1. That unsanitary or unsafe inhabited dwelling accommodation exist within the City of Rector, Arkansas.
1. That there is a shortage of safe or sanitary dwelling accommodations in the City of Rector, Arkansas, available to persons of low income at rentals they can afford.
{: .ordinance-list-lower-alpha}

Whereas, the Mayor of the City of Rector from this day forth shall appoint five (5) commissioners to serve on the Housing Authority. The commissioners appointed after this resolution is passed shall be designated to serve terms of one (1), two (2), three (3), four (4) and five (5) years, respectively, from the date of their appointment, but thereafter commissioners shall be appointed as aforesaid for a term of office of five (5) years. Said appointments shall be made with the approval of City Council. If, for any reason, the Mayor does not notify said City Council of any person to be appointed, the previous appointee shall continue to hold office until a successor is named. Said commission shall comply with Arkansas Statute 19-3005 through 19-3074 inclusive.

Now therefore, be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, that this Resolution was passed by the City Council this 1st day of August, 1972.

H. R. French, Mayor

Attest: Lavern Manchester, City Recorder
