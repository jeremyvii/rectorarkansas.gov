**An ordinance to regulate the monthly meetings of the Rector City Council and to regulate special meetings providing for an emergency.**

Whereas: The Rector City Council, has regularly met on the 3rd Thursday of each month for the regular meetings.

Whereas: The Rector City Council, has decided that it would be in the best interest that the City Council meet on the 1st Monday of each month at 7:00 p.m.

Therefore, effective May 1995, the regular meeting of the City Council shall meet on the 1st Monday of each month at 7:00 p.m.

## Section 1.

That the Mayor may call a special meeting at any time that he may deem proper in the best interest of the City of Rector and shall give notice to all the City Council Members and members of the press as required under the freedom information act.

This ordinance being necessary for the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Rector and necessary for effective meeting, there being a significant scheduling problem for members of the City Council within the City of Rector, an emergency is declared to exist and this ordinance shall take effect and being in force from and after its passage.

Passed this 1st day of May, 1995.

Ronald E. Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Kenneth R. Burns, City Recorder
