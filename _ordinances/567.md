**An ordinance amending ordinance 510 for the City of Rector water and sewage deposit amounts, and for other purposes**

Whereas, the City of Rector incurs great costs in operating the water and sewer in the City of Rector and has not issued an increase in deposits for residential customers in several years and is therefore losing money and unable to operate to serve citizens of Rector; and

Whereas, it has been determined by the Rector City Council that the deposit amount set in 2006 shall be revisited and increased to $150.00, and

Whereas, it has been determined that the rising costs of water and sewer treatment as well as viability of the water system necessitates an increase in the deposit amount.

Now therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows:

## Section 1.

It has been determined that section 2 of Ordinance 510 shall be revised as follows:

The water and sewer deposit for new residential customers shall be $150.00.

## Section 2.

All Ordinances and parts of Ordinances in conflict within the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. Unless specifically amended herein, all other portions of previous water and sewage Ordinances shall remain in full force and effect.

Passed and approved on this 5th day of August, 2019.

Mayor Teresa Roofe
