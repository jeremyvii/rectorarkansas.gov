**An ordinance amending Ordinances No. 391 and 397 of the City of Rector, Arkansas, for the purpose of fixing new rates for deposit, charges and tapping fees; and declaring an emergency.**

Whereas, the City of Rector has deemed it necessary to increase and/or adjust fees for deposit charges and tapping fees charged to customers served by the Water and Sewer System of the City of Rector;

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas that Section 3 of Ordinance 397 as amended by Ordinance 391 is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Section 3.

The City of Rector hereby established and fixes the following deposit charges, tapping fees and service charges applicable to water and sewer customers, to-wit:

### Deposits

| User | Deposit |
| --- | --- |
| Residential Users | $50.00 |
| Commercial Users | 50.00 |
| Industrial Users and Schools | 100.00 |

### Tapping fees

The fees charged for all sizes of water and sewing tapping will be cost plus labor.

### Service charges

| Service | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| Disconnect | No Charge |
| Reconnect | $15.00 |

## Emergency clause.

The City Council hereby determines that the City's present water and sewer fees in connection with deposits and tapping fees do not adequately protect the City for the reasons that such charges are made by the City and do not presently cover the City's actual expenditures for such services and in order enable the City to provide adequate public water and sewer services and in order to provide for the services essential to the public welfare; that the immediate passage of this Ordinance is necessary to ensure the provision of adequate public water and sewer services. Therefore, an emergency is hereby declared to exist and this Ordinance be necessary for the public health, safety and welfare, shall take effect and be in full force from and after it's passage and publication.

Passed and approved this 9th day of [illegible], 1994.

Approved: Reuben O. Seay, Mayor

Attest: La Vonda Benson, City Clerk

Seal
