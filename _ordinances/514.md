An ordinance amending ordinance no. 497 passed and approved May 5, 2004 and for other purposes

Be it ordanined by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas:

That Ordinance No. 497 is hereby amended as follows:

## Section 1.:

Section 5 is hereby amended to read as follows:

The first violation of any provision of this Ordinance shall be punishable by a fine of $100.00; a second violation within 12 months shall be punishable by a fine of $200.00; and the third violation within 12 months shall be punishable by a fine of $500.00. Further, each violation is respective only to the person and not to individual properties, i.e., the violation will run with the person and not to the respective property only.

All other portions of Section 5 of Ordinance No. 497 shall remain in full force and effect.

## Section 2.:

It is determined by the City of Rector due to the need for matters to be resolved in upcoming district court, therefore that an emergency is declared to exist and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon it’s passage

## Section 3.:

All Ordinances and parts of Ordinances in conflict within the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. Unless specifically amended herein, all other portions of Ordinance No. 497 shall remain in full force and effect.

Passed and approved on this 5th day of June, 2006.

Mayor Ron Kemp
