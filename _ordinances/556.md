**An ordinance hereby increasing the pay for officials, and declaring an emergency, and for other purposes.**

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas as follows:

Whereas: In review of pay schedules of similarly situation cities of the 2nd Class and budgetary revisions, and to more adequately align the elected officials pay with those of similar cities; and

Whereas: It is determined that the City shall increase the pay for the elected officials, and such shall be done prior to a new term of office for the elected officials;

Now therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows:

## Section 1.

 It has been determined that effective January 1, 2015, the City of Rector shall provide the following salaries for the following positions:

<table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Position</th><th scope="col">Salary</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><th scope="row">Mayor</th><td>$1250.00 monthly</td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row">Recorder/Treasurer</th><td>$520.00 monthly</td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row">City Attorney</th><td>$1030.00 monthly</td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row">Alderman/Council Members</th><td>$200.00 monthly</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

## Section 2:

All Ordinances and parts of Ordinances in conflict within the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. Unless specifically amended herein, all other portions of Ordinances shall remain in full force and effect.

## Section 3:

The City of Rector is in immediate need for such a revision for the citizens of the City of Rector, Arkansas, due to the upcoming budget, changes in elected officials, and terms beginning in 2015, therefore, an emergency is declared to exist and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage, approval, and publication as required by law.

Passed and approved this 1st day of Dec., 2014

G. David Freeman

Mayor
