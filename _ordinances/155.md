**An ordinance laying off certain portions of the city of Rector, Arkansas, into a Street Improvement District and designating the boundaries thereof. Said district to be known as Street Improvement District No. 2 of the City of Rector, Arkansas.**

Whereas, ten owners of real property within the boundaries hereinafter mentioned and described, have petitioned the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, to take steps towards the making of local improvements, therein, by graveling and curbing and all things necessary to the complete gravelling improving, including grading and surfacing thereof, upon and along that part of the following street, within said city, to-wit:

That part of Fourth Street commencing at the East line of the Right-of-way of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad and running thence East to the corporation line of the city of Rector, Arkansas.

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

## Section 1.

That Street Improvement District No. 2 of the City of Rector, Arkansas, be and the same is hereby created and established for the purpose of graveling and curbing and all things necessary to the complete graveling improving, including grading and surfacing thereof, upon and along the following street, to-wit:

That part of Fourth Street, commencing at the East line of the Right-of-way of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad and running thence East to the corporation line of the City of Rector, Arkansas, and the boundaries of said Improvement District shall be as follows, to-wit:

## Section 2.

Commencing at the Northeast corner of Lot 12, Block 1, Daniel's Addition to the Town of Rector, and running thence North two hundred feet; thence West to the Northeast corner of Lot 7, Block 3 of R. A. C. Wright's Addition to the town of Rector, thence West along the North end of Lots 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, in Block 3, and along the North end of Lots 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, in Block 4 of R. A. C. Wright's Addition to the town of Rector, thence due West to intersect with the East line of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad Right-of-way, thence in a Southwestern direction along the right-of-way of said Railroad to the center of the West line of Block 33, thence East to the Southwest corner of Lot 3, Block 38, of S. Imp. Association's Addition, to the Town of Rector, thence along the South line of Lots 3 & 10, Block 38, S. Imp. Association Addition, and on East along the South line of Lots 3 & 10, Block 39, S. Imp. Association Addition, to the town of Rector, thence east along the South end of Lots 12, 11, 10, 9, 8, 7, Block 1, R.A.C. Wright's Addition to the Town of Rector, thence on East along the South end of Lots 12, 11, 10, 9, 8, 7, Block 2, R.A.C. Wright's Addition, thence on East along the South ends of Lots 22, 21, 20, 19, 18, 17, 16, 15, 14, 13, 12 Block 1, Daniels Addition to the town of Rector, thence North along the East line of Lot 12, Block 1 of Daniels Addition, to the place of beginning, all within the corporate limits of the city of Rector, Arkansas. Said District to be known as Street Improvement District No. 2 of the city of Rector,Arkansas, for the purpose of building, constructing and maintaining gravel and graveling improvements, curbing, and to do all things necessary to the complete graveling, improving, grading and surfacing thereof, out of such gravel and in such manner as the commissioners, hereinafter appointed may determine.

## Section 3.

All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed and this ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage and the City Recorder, is hereby ordered, within twenty days after the passage of this ordinance, to publish the same in some newspaper published in Clay County, Arkansas.

Passed and approved this the 6th day of July, 1925.

/s D. G. Moseley  
Mayor

Signed: R. E. French  
Recorder
