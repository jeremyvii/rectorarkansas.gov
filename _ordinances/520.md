**An Ordinance Vacating An Alley Beginning at the Northwest Corner of Lot 1 run thence South to the Southwest Corner of Lot 6, run thence West to the Southeast Corner of Lot 7, run thence North to the Northeast Corner of Lot 12 run thence East to the point of beginning, located in Block 1 of McNeil’s Addition.**

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows, to-wit:

Wherefore, a petition was duly filed with the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, on the 5th day of March, 2007, asking the City Council to vacate and abandon all that portion of an alley designated as an alley Beginning at the Northwest Corner of Lot 1 run thence South to the Southwest Corner of Lot 6, run thence West to the Southeast Corner of Lot 7, run thence North to the Northeast Corner of Lot 12 run thence East to the point of beginning, located in Block 1 of McNeil’s Addition.

Whereas, after due notice as required by law, the City Council has, at the time and place mentioned in the notice, heard all persons desiring to be heard on the question and has ascertained that an alley or the portion thereof, hereinbefore described, has heretofore been dedicated to the public use as a alley herein described; that said alley has not been actually used by the public generally for a period of at least five years subsequent to the filing of the plat; that all the owner of the property abutting upon the portion of an alley to be vacated has filed with the Council her written consent to such abandonment; and the public interest and welfare will not be adversely affected by the abandonment of such street.

Now therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows, to-wit:

## Section 1.

The City of Rector, Arkansas, hereby releases, vacates and abandons all its rights, together with the rights of the public generally, in and to an alley designated as follows:

Beginning at the Northwest Corner of Lot 1 run thence South to the Southwest Corner of Lot 6, run thence West to the Southeast Corner of Lot 7, run thence North to the Northeast Corner of Lot 12 run thence East to the point of beginning, located in Block 1 of McNeil’s Addition.

## Section 2.

A copy of the ordinance duly certified by the City Recorder shall be filed in the Office of the Circuit Clerk and Ex-Officio Recorder of the county and recorder in the deed records for the Eastern District of Clay County.

## Section 3.

This ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage.

Passed and approved by the City Council of the City of Rector, this 5th day of March, 2007.

Ron Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Lowell D. McKenney, City Clerk

**In the matter of the vacating of a street in the City of Rector, Arkansas located in the McNiel’S addition**
