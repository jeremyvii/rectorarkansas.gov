**The abandonment of a portion of Mary Street and Velta Street**

The South Half of Mary Street and the East Half of Velta Street as shown on record plat of Marlar’s Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas, lying adjacent to Lots 6 & 7, and the West 12.5 feet of Lots 5 & 8, Block 1 of Marlar’s Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas, described as follows: Beginning at the Southwest corner of said Lot 7, run thence North 0 degrees 18’ W 211.0 feet, run thence North 89 degrees 42’ E 75.0 feet, run thence North 0 degrees 18’ W 20.0 feet, run thence South 89 degrees 42’ W 93.0 feet, run thence South 0 degrees 18’ E 231.0 feet, run thence North 89 degrees 42’ E 18.0 feet to the true point of beginning.

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows, to-wit:

Wherefore, a petition was duly filed with the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, on the 22nd day of August, 2007, asking the City Council to vacate and abandon all that portion of an street designated as Mary Street and Velta Street, described as The South Half of Mary Street and the East Half of Velta Street as shown on record plat of Marlar’s Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas, lying adjacent to Lots 6 & 7, and the West 12.5 feet of Lots 5 & 8, Block 1 of Marlar’s Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas, described as follows: Beginning at the Southwest corner of said Lot 7, run thence North 0 degrees 18’ W 211.0 feet, run thence North 89 degrees 42’ E 75.0 feet, run thence North 0 degrees 18’ W 20.0 feet, run thence South 89 degrees 42’ W 93.0 feet, run thence South 0 degrees 18’ E 231.0 feet, run thence North 89 degrees 42’ E 18.0 feet to the true point of beginning.

Whereas, after due notice as required by law, the City Council has, at the time and place mentioned in the notice, heard all persons desiring to be heard on the question and has ascertained that the streets or the portion thereof, hereinbefore described, have heretofore been dedicated to the public use as street herein described; that said streets have not been actually used by the public generally for a period of at least five years subsequent to the filing of the plat; that the owner of the property abutting upon the portion of the streets to be vacated has filed with the Council his written Petition to such abandonment; and the public interest and welfare will not be adversely affected by the abandonment of a portion of such streets.

Now therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows, to-wit:

## Section 1.

The City of Rector, Arkansas, hereby releases, vacates and abandons all its rights, together with the rights of the public generally, in and to a portion of streets designated as follows:

The South Half of Mary Street and the East Half of Velta Street as shown on record plat of Marlar’s Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas, lying adjacent to Lots 6 & 7, and the West 12.5 feet of Lots 5 & 8, Block 1 of Marlar’s Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas, described as follows: Beginning at the Southwest corner of said Lot 7, run thence North 0 degrees 18’ W 211.0 feet, run thence North 89 degrees 42’ E 75.0 feet, run thence North 0 degrees 18’ W 20.0 feet, run thence South 89 degrees 42’ W 93.0 feet, run thence South 0 degrees 18’ E 231.0 feet, run thence North 89 degrees 42’ E 18.0 feet to the true point of beginning.

## Section 2.

A copy of the ordinance duly certified by the City Recorder shall be filed in the Office of the Circuit Clerk and Ex-Officio Recorder of the county and recorder in the deed records for the Eastern District of Clay County.

## Section 3.

This ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage.

Passed and approved by the City Council of the City of Rector, this 10th day of September, 2007.

Ron Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Lowell D. McKenney, City Clerk
