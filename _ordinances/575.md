**An ordinance to restrict materials or obstructions in the roadway of the City of Rector, State of Arkansas, and for other purposes**

Whereas, it has come to the attention of the Rector Police Department regarding problems with debris causing obstructions in roadways in the City of Rector, and

Whereas, provisions should be in place to prevent obstructions for vision of traffic in the City of Rector, and

Whereas, material that may cause damage to persons or property should be removed from the roadways in the City.

Now therefore be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas as follows to wit:

## Section 1:

Any person who drops or permits to be dropped or thrown upon any street any noxious, destructive or injurious material shall immediately remove the same.

## Section 2:

Any person authorized to remove a wrecked or damaged vehicle from a street shall remove any glass or other injurious substance dropped upon the street from such vehicle.

## Section 3:

No person shall place any obstruction in or upon a street without proper authority.

## Section 4:

No person shall place snow, ice, grass clipping, or other debris upon the traveled portion of any street, highway or alley, or within an intersection as to obstruct vision of traffic or any traffic control devices.

## Section 5:

Whoever violates any provision of is guilty of a misdemeanor and shall be fined $200.00. If, within three years of the offense, the offender previously has been convicted of or pleaded guilty to the offense, subsequent offense shall be fined $400.00 The offender shall also pay any restitution or other fees as the court may deem necessary.

## Section 6:

All Ordinances and parts of Ordinances in conflict within the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed.

In witness whereof, said ordinance being duly passed by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, this 1st day of May, 2023.

Mayor: Shawn Brandon

City Clerk: Debi Boyd
