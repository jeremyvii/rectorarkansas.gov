**An ordinance to amend ordinance #359 to establish regulations for manufactured home installation**

Whereas, it has come to the attention of the City Council for the City of Rector, Arkansas that regulations be implemented for the placing of manufactured homes within the City of Rector.

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows, to-wit:

That Ordinance #359 of the City of Rector is hereby amended to add the section herein below to the existing regulation Article 5-3, section B, sub-section B to read as follows:

### B.

Manufactured homes installed in R-2 Zones in the City of Rector shall have a perimeter foundation composed of concrete, masonry, or vinyl or metal skirting, designed and manufactured for use as a perimeter foundation for manufactured homes only, installed and ventilated in accordance with the manufactures installation instruction for the home.

## Emergency clause:

This ordinance being necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health, comfort, convenience, morals, safety and welfare of the City of Rector, Arkansas, an emergency is declared to exist and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect from the date of its adoption.

In witness whereof, said Ordinance being duly passed by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, this 6th day of Oct., 1997.

City of Rector, Arkansas

By: Ron Kemp, Mayor

Attested: Kenneth R. Burns

Kenneth Burns, Secretary/Treasurer
