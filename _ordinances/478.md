**An ordinance to amend ordinance no. 359 to establish a setback line within the zoning area of the City of Rector, Arkansas.**

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows to-wit,:

Whereas: the City of Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, has determined that the original setback line defined in the original ordinance was as follows:

## Section 1. Current requirements:

R1. Front yard setback should be a minimum of 30 feet from front property line, or 50 feet from center line of street, whichever is greater.

R2. Front yard setback should be a minimum of minimum of 25 feet from front property line or 50 feet from center line of street, whichever is greater.

## Section 2. Proposed area requirements:

R1 and R2: Front yard setback to be a minimum of 20 feet from the property line.

## Section 3.

All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed.

## Section 4.

The City Clerk or Enforcement Officer is hereby directed to amend the official zoning map of the City of Rector, Arkansas, insofar as it relates to the lands described above so that the zoning thereof shall be in accordance with the provisions of this ordinance.

## Section 5:

An emergency is declared to exist by the City Council within the City of Rector and the passage of this ordinance is necessary for the preservation of public health, maintaining safety and public peace of the citizens of Rector. The members of the City Council hereby unanimously and expressly dispense with the rule requiring the reading of an ordinance of three different dates before its passage thereof, and this ordinance shall take effect and be enforced from and after its passage.

Passed and approved this 3rd day of December, 2001.

By: Mayor Ronald E. Kemp

Attest: Recorder/Treasurer Kenneth R. Burns
