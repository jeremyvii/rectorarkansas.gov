**An Ordinance, "Entitled an ordinance to annex contiguous Territory to the incorporated Town of Rector."**

Be it ordained by the Town Council of the Incorporated Town of Rector.

## Sec. 1.

That pursuant to an order of the County Court of Clay County, Arkansas, made on the 14th day of October, 1908, in the matter of annexing certain territory to the Incorporated Town of Rector, said order having been duly certified to the Council on the 17th day of October, 1908, the territory therein described, to-wit: a part of the North half of Section 26, Township 19 North, Range 7 East, in Clay County, Arkansas, bounded as follows: Beginning at the Southwest corner of the Incorporated Town of Rector, as the boundary line existed before theannexation herein referred to, thence South 40 rods, thence east about 200 rods to the Southeast Corner of the Northwest quarter of the Northeast Quarter of said Section, thence north to the South boundary line of said Town as the same existed before the annexation herein referred to, thence west to the place of beginning, that is to say, the South-half of the Northwest Quarter of the Northeast Quarter, the South-half of the Northeast Quarter of the Northwest Quarter and the Southeast Quarter of the Northwest Quarter of the Northwest Quarter of said Section 26, be and the same is hereby annexed to the incorporated Town of Rector.

## Sec. 2.

The jurisdiction of the Incorporated Town of Rector is hereby extended over the said annexed territory for all Civil, Criminal and Political purposes whatsoever.

## Sec. 3.

This Ordinance shall go into force and effect from and after its passage.

Passed and approved Nov. 23rd, 1908.
