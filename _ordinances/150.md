**An Ordinance laying off certain portions of the City of Rector, Arkansas, into a Street Improvement District and designating the boundaries thereof. Said District to be known as Street Improvement District No. 1 of the City of Rector, Arkansas.**

Whereas ten owners of real property within the boundaries hereinafter mentioned and described, have petitioned the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, to take steps towards the making of local improvements, therein, by paving, curbing, guttering and storm sewering and all things necessary to the complete paving improving including grading and surfacing thereof, upon and along that part of the following streets and alleys, within said City, to-wit:

Commencing at the west line of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad, Right-of-way, on Main Street and running thence North to the Southeast corner of Lot 4 in Block 10 of the Original Town of Rector, and that part of Third Street commencing with the east line of Dodd Street and running thence east to and across Main Street to the Southeast corner of Lot One in Block 11 of the Original Town of Rector, and that part of Fourth Street commencing with the East line of Dodd Street and running thence East to and across Main Street to the west line of the Right-of-way of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad, and that part of Fifth Street commencing with the east line of Dodd Street and running thence East to and across Main Street to the West line of the Right-of-way of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad, all of Front Street and that part of the street parallell with the Right-of-way of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad, from the North line of Fifth Street to the South line of Fourth Street, and that part of Stewart's Avenue from Front Street to the Southeast corner of Lot 15 in Block Six of the Original Town of Rector, and alleys within said District herein after described.

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

## Section 1.

That Street Improvement District No. 1, of the City of Rector, Arkansas, be and the same is hereby created and established for the purpose of paving, curbing, guttering and storm sewering and all things necessary to the complete paving improving, including grading and surfacing thereof, upon and along the following streets and alleys, to-wit:

Commencing at the west line of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad Right-of-way, on Main Street, and running thence North to the Southeast corner of Lot 4, in Block 10 of the Original Town of Rector, and that part of Third Street commencing with the east line of Dodd Street and running thence East to and across Main Street to the Southeast corner of Lot one on Block 11, of the Original Town of Rector, and that part of Fourth Street commencing with the East line of Dodd Street and running thence East to and across Main Street to the west line of the Right-of-way of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad, and that part of Fifth Street commencing with the east line of Dodd Street and running thence East to and across Main Street to the West line of the Right-of-way of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad, all of Front Street and that part of the street parallel with the right-of-way of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad, from the north line of Fifth Street to the South line of Fourth Street, and that part of Stewart's Avenue, from Front Street to the Southeast corner of Lot 15 in Block 6 of the original Town of Rector, and the alleys within said District hereinafter described. The boundaries of said Improvement District shall be as follows, to-wit:

## Section 2.

Beginning at a point where the North line of Sixth Street intersects the east boundary line of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad Right-of-way, thence West along the north line of said Sixth Street to the Southeast corner of Lot 12, in Block One of the Original Town of Rector, thence North along the east line of Lots 12, 11 and 10, to the South line of Lot 9, in Block One of the Original Town of Rector, thence West along the South line of Lot 9 in Block One of the Original Town of Rector, thence West along the South line of Lot 9 to the East line of Dodd Street, thence North along the East line of Dodd Street to the South line of Lot 9, in Block 10 of the Original Town of Rector, thence East along the south line of Lots 9 and 4, in Block 10, and across Main Street and along the South line of Lot 4, to the Southeast corner of Lot 4 in Block 11, of the Original Town of Rector, thence South along the east line of Lots 3, 2 and 1, in Block 11, and thence on South along the east line of Lots 12, 11, 10, 9, 8 and 7, in Block 6, thence due East to the East boundary ofn the Right-of-way of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad, thence in a Southwesterly direction with the east boundary line of said Railroad, to the point of beginning, all within the corporate limits of the City of Rector, Arkansas. Said District to be known as Paving District No. 1 of the City of Rector, Arkansas, for the purpose of building, constructing, laying and maintaining pavements and paving improvements, curbing, gutters, storm sewers, and to do all things necessary to the complete paving improving, grading and surfacing thereof, out of such material and in such manner as the commissioners, hereinafter appointed, may determine.

## Section 3.

That all ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed and this ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage and the City Recorder is hereby ordered, within twenty days after the passage of this Ordinance, to publish the same in some newspaper published in Clay County, Arkansas.

Passed and approved this the 1st day of December, 1924.

/s D. G. Moseley
