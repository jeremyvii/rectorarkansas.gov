**Ordinance to amend the zoning ordinances of the City of Rector, Arkansas, and for other purposes**

Whereas: It has come to the attention of the City of Rector Council Members that it would be in the best interest of the citizens of Rector that the Zoning be revised.

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the City of Rector Council Members as follows, to wit:

That Ordinance No. [blank] Section C, Paragraph 4, 5, & 6 is hereby amended to revise as follows:

1. Zoning for the use of free standing pre manufactured metal carports will require two (2) feet setback side on R2 and R1 lots with the front yard setback to remain the same.
1. Zoning for the rear yard setback on R2 and R1 lots to be a minimum of 10 feet from rear property line.
1. Zoning for the interior and exterior lot of side yard line on R2 and R1 to be a minimum of ten (10) feet from the property line.
{: .ordinance-list-upper-alpha}

## Section 2:

This ordinance being necessary for the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Rector and necessary for effective meeting, there being a significant scheduling problem for members of the City Council within the City of Rector, an emergency is declared to exist and this ordinance shall take effect and being in force and after its passage and publication.

Passed and approved this 4th day of December, 2000.

Mayor Ronald E. Kemp

Attest: Recorder/Treasurer Kenneth R. Burns
