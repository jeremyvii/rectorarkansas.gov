**An ordinance to adopt new district rates for the rural fire district, repealing all other ordinances in conflict therewith, declaring an emergency and for other purposes.**

Whereas, from time to time it is necessary for the city to review rates as it relates to the Rural Fire District to better provide for the safety and welfare of its citizens; and

Whereas, the City has adopted the following proposed rates for members in the Rural Fire District for 2014:

<ol style="list-style-type: none;">
<li>1)
<ol style="list-style-type: lower-alpha;">
<li>Residential - $45.00 yearly (Structures on contiguous property that primary residential structure is located on.)</li>
<li>Farm/Business - $45.00 yearly (Structures on contiguous property as main office.)</li>
<li>Rental Property - $45.00 each specified single rental property. Each additional specified property is $45.00. This does not apply to vehicles or farm implements;</li>
<li>All inclusive Farm/Business - $200.00 annually. Applies to all property, vehicles, and implements if the incident occurs in our district.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li>2) The proposed expenses that will be charged to non-members in the Rural Fire District will be as follows:
<ol style="list-style-type: lower-alpha;">
<li>Real Estate (structures) - $800.00</li>
<li>Brush and not involving a structure - $350.00</li>
<li>Personal property - $350.00.</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>

Now therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows:

## Section 1:

The proposed rates for 2014 of the Rural Fire District is hereby adopted as the rates of the City of Rector, Arkansas for all purposes contained therein.

## Section 2:

That all ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed.

## Section 3:

That the Rector City Clerk is hereby ordered and directed to cause this ordinance to be published.

## Section 4:

That in order to maintain public safety and assist in providing the best fire protection possible to the citizens of Rector, an emergency is declared and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after the date of its passage and publication.

Passed and approved this 2nd day of Dec., 2013.

G. David Freeman
