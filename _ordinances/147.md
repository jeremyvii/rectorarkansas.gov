**An Ordinance Laying Off and Creating an Improvement District for the purpose of purchasing, erecting and installing a light and power plant with poles, lines and all things necessary for the generating and transmission of electrical current for use by Subscribers.**

Whereas more than ten owners of Real Property in the City of Rector have petitioned the City Council of the City of Rector to lay off and create an improvement district in the City of Rector covering the whole of the territory within the corporate limits of said City of Rector into an improvement district for the purpose of purchasing, erecting and installing a light and powr plant with poles, lines and all other accessories necessary for the generating and transmission of electrical current for use by subscribers.

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

## Section 1.

That the whole of the territory within the corporate limits of the City of Rector, Arkansas, bounded as follows, to-wit: Commencing at the corners of 23, 24, 25 and 26, Township Nineteen North, Range Seven East, and running thence North seven-eights of a mile, thence West one-half of a mile, thence South one-eights of a mile, thence West five-eighths of a mile, thence South three-fourths of a mile, thence East one-fourth of a mile, thence South one-fourth of a mile, thence East five-eighths of a mile, thence North one-eights of a mile, thence East one-eighth of a mile, thence North one-eighth of a mile, thence East one-eighth of a mile to the place of beginning be and the same is hereby laid off into an improvement district for the purpose of purchasing, erecting and installing a light and power plant with poles, lines and all accessories for the purpose of generating and the transmition of electrical current for distribution to subscribers and for use by the said City of Rector.

That the Corporate limits of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as herein before described, be and they are hereby deisnged as the boundaries of said District, and the territory embraced therein shall be known as Light and Power Improvement District No. 1 of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

## Section 2.

That all ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith be and the same is hereby repealed and this ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its publication.

Approved this the 17th day of December 1923.

/s D. G. Moseley  
Mayor

Attest: /s R. E. French
