**An ordinance authorizing the City of Rector, Arkansas, to transact and conduct business with the Clay County Democrat and for other purposes.**

Whereas, it is necessary for the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, to transact or conduct business with a newspaper and/or publishing company which is published or circulated in Clay County, Arkansas, which has a bona fide circulation in said County; and

Whereas, the Clay County Democrat offers said services to the citizens of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

Therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows:

## Section 1.:

It is determined by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas to acquire the above described services with the Clay County Democrat.

Passed and approved on this 18th day of December, 2006.

Ronald E. Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Lowell D. McKenney, City Clerk
