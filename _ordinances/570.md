**An ordinance establishing court sessions for the City of Rector in the 17th State Judicial District, and for other purposes**

Whereas, the City of Rector is a designated department of the 17th State Judicial District as set forth in Ark. Code Section 16-10-1113(i)(2)(B), and

Whereas, upon consideration, the City Council and the 17th State Judicial District Judge mutually agree to waive the requirement that the district judge hold court in the Rector Department each month as required by Ark. Code Ann. Section 16-17-138.

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, State of Arkansas, as follows:

## Section 1.

Court sessions pertaining to the City of Rector will be held at Clay County District Court, Eastern District in Piggott, Arkansas.

## Section 2. Emergency

It is hereby found and determined by the City Council that an emergency is hereby declared to exist, and this Ordinance, being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health and safety, shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage and approval.

Passed and approved this 2nd day of May, 2022.

Approved: Teresa L. Roofe, Mayor

Attest: Shelia Dale
