**Entitled an ordinance requiring each Telegraph, Telephone or Electric Light Co. to file with the City Recorder a sworn statement of number of poles used by said company within the City limits and for other purposes.**

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

## Section 1.

The secretary of each Telegraph, Telephone and Electric Light Company is hereby required to file with the City Recorder of the City of Rector a sworn statement of the number of poles used by such company within the City limits whether rented, leased or owned by said company and such statement shall be furnish on or before the Fifteenth (15th) day of September of each year.

## Section 2.

Each such Telephone, Telegraph and Electric Light Company shall annually pay a sum equal to Twenty-five cents (25¢) for each pole used within the City limits of the City of Rector whether same is rented, leased or owned by said company.

## Section 3.

If any such company shall violate any of the provisions of section one and two (1 & 2) of this ordinance such company shall upon conviction thereof, be fined in any sum not to exceed $200.00 Two Hundred Dollars and each day said company shall continue to violate said sections One (1) and Two (2) shall be and constitute a separate offense.

## Section 4.

This ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage.

Passed and approved this 2nd day of December, 1918.

Attest: T. A. Janes,  
Recorder

Signed: J. W. Dollison,  
Mayor
