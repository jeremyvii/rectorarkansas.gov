**An Ordinance laying off and Creating an Improvement District for the purpose of grading and paving the streets and allies within said District.**

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

## Section 1.

That the territory lying within the boundaries hereinafter described, be and the same is hereby laid off into an improvement district for the purpose of grading and paving the streets and allies in said district and doing all things necessary to carry out the purpose of said improvement District.

That the following described boundaries: Beginning at a point where the North line of Sixth Street intersects the East boundary line of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad right-of-way and running thence West along the North line of Sixth street to the Southeast corner of Lot 12 in Block No. 1 in the Original Town of Rector, thence North along the East line of Lots 12, 11, and 10 to the Southeast corner of Lots 9 in said Block 1, thence West the South line of Lot 9 to the East line of Dodd Street, thence North along the East line of Dodd Street to the Southwest corner of Lot 9 in Block 10 in the Original Town of Rector, thence East across Block 10, along the South line of Lots 9 and 4 in said Block 10 and on East across Main Street and along the South line of Lot 4 in Block 11 to the Southeast corner of said Lot 4 in Block 11 in the Original Town of Rector, thence South along the East line of Lots 3, 2 and 1 in said Block 11 to and across third street and along South along the East line of Lots 12, 11, 10, 9, 8 and 7 to the Southeast corner of said Lot 7 in Block 6 of the Original Town of Rector, thence due East to the East Boundary line of the St. Louis and Southwestern Railroad right-of-way, thence in a Southwesterly direction along the East line of said right-of-way to the place of beginning, be and the same is hereby designated as the boundary lines of said improvement district, and the territory embraced therein shall be known as Paving Improvement District No. 1. of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

## Section 2.

That all ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith be and the same are hereby repealed and this ordinance shall be in force from and after its publication.

Approved this the 2nd day of June 1924.

s/ D. G. Moseley  
Mayor
