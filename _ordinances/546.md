**Ordinance to amend the zoning ordinances of the City of Rector, Arkansas, and for other purposes**

Whereas, under the authority of the zoning ordinances of the city of Rector, Arkansas, the following described land is zoned R-2

That part of Lots Three, Four, Five and six (3,4,5,&6), all in block One(1) of W.Howard’s Addition to the town of Rector, Arkansas, described as follows: Beginning at the Northwest corner of said Block One (1), run thence South 178', run thence East 85', run thence North 40.5', run thence North 87degrees 17', East 55', run thence North 38', run thence South 88 degrees 45' West 43', run thence north 100', run thence south 88 degrees 45' west 97' to the point of beginning.

And

The East Forty-three (43) feet off of and across the entire East ends of lots Five and Six (5&6) in Block one (1) in Howard’s Addition to the Town of Rector, Clay County, Arkansas.

Whereas, The above described land is now owned by Josh Jackson, and rezoning of the said land to commercial C-2 will promote and benefit the economy of the city and provide the most efficient use of the above described land; and whereas, the planning commission of the City of Rector,Arkansas, has recommended the adoption of this ordinance to the city council; and whereas, it appears to the city council that all applicable laws of the state of Arkansas and of the city have been complied with in presenting this ordinance to the city council;

Now therefore, be it ordained by the city council of Rector, Arkansas;

## Section 1:

The Zoning Ordinances of the City of Rector,Arkansas,should be and they hereby are amended so that the land hereinafter described is zoned Commercial C-2,to-wit:

That part of Lots Three, Four, Five and six (3,4,5,&6), all in block One(1) of W.Howard’s Addition to the town of Rector, Arkansas, described as follows: Beginning at the Northwest corner of said Block One (1), run thence South 178', run thence East 85', run thence North 40.5', run thence North 87degrees 17', East 55', run thence North 38', run thence South 88 degrees 45' West 43', run thence north 100', run thence south 88 degrees 45' west 97' to the point of beginning.

And

The East Forty-three (43) feet off of and across the entire East ends of lots Five and Six (5&6) in Block one (1) in Howard’s Addition to the Town of Rector, Clay County, Arkansas.

## Section 2:

All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed.

## Section 3:

The City Clerk is hereby directed to amend the official zoning map of the City of Rector, Arkansas,insofar as it relates to the lands described above so that the zoning thereof shall be in accordance with the provisions of this ordinance.

Passed and approved this 7th day of March 2011

By: Mayor G. David Freeman

Attest: Recorder/Treasurer Lowell D. McKenney
