**"An Ordinance licensing auctioneers."**

"Be it ordained by the Council of the Incorporated Town of Rector."

## Sec. 1.

Before any person or persons shall engage in auctioning goods of any kind, where any compensation is received therefor, such person or persons shall first procure a license, to be issued by the Recorder and countersigned by the Marshall, authorizing such person or persons to engage in such business, and for which license the applicant shall pay a fee of Seven Dollars and Fifty Cents ($7.50) into the Town Treasury as a police regulation together with a Recorder's fee of Two dollars ($2.00) and a Marshall's fee of Fifty cents ($.50).

## Sec. 2.

If any person or persons shall auction or attempt to auction any goods or chattels whatever, except the owner or owners of the goods auctioned without first complying with Section 1 of this ordinance shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be fined in any sum not to exceed Twenty-five Dollars ($25.00)

## Sec. 3.

All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed and this ordinance shall take effect upon its passage and be in force thereafter.

Passed and approved January 3rd, 1905.

Signed: W. E. Lynch  
Recorder

Signed: Frank Weldin  
Mayor
