Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkanasas, as follows, to-wit:

Wherefore, a petition was duly filed with the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, on the 1st day of July, 2024, asking the city Council to vacate and abandon all that portion of an alley designated an alley on That part of Block 1 and Block 2 all in G W Winn's Addition, Rector described as follows; Beginning at the Southwest corner of Block 2 of G W Winn's Addition; thence N00°42'27"W along the West Line of Block 1 & 2, 470.00 feet to the North line of Lot 11 in Block 1; thence N89°32'15"E along said North line 150.00 feet to the Centerline of the (20 foot) Alley; thence S00°42'27"E along said centerline, 260.00 feet to the centerline of the Alley running between Block 1 and Block 2; thence N89°32'15"E along said centerline, 160.00 feet to the projection of the East line of Block 2; thence S00°42'27"E along said East line, 210.00 feet to the South line of Block 2; thence S89°32'15"W along said South line, 320.00 feet to the true point of beginning, containing 2.50 acres more or less, now appearing of record in Deed Record 2 at page 130 in the office of the Circuit Clerk and Ex-Officio Recorder for the Eastern District of Clay County, Arkansas.

Whereas, after due notice as required by law, the City Council has, at the time and place mentioned in the notice, heard all persons desiring to be heard on the question and has ascertained that an alley or the portion thereto, hereinbefore described, has heretofore been dedicated to the public use as an alley herein described; that said alley has not been actually used by the public generally for a period of at least five years subsequent to the filing of the plat; that all the owner os the property abutting upon the portion of an alley to be vacated has filed with Council its written consent to such abandonment; and the public interest and welfare will not be adversely affected by the abandonment of such alley.

## Section 1:

The City of Rector, Arkansas, hereby releases, vacates and abandons all its rights, together with the rights of public generally, in and to an alley crossing the real property designated as follows:

That part of Block 1 and Block 2 all in G W Winn's Addition, Rector described as follows; Beginning at the Southwest corner of Block 2 of G W Winn's Addition; thence N00°42'27"W along the West Line of Block 1 & 2, 470.00 feet to the North line of Lot 11 in Block 1; thence N89°32'15"E along said North line 150.00 feet to the Centerline of the (20 foot) Alley; thence S00°42'27"E along said centerline, 260.00 feet to the centerline of the Alley running between Block 1 and Block 2; thence N89°32'15"E along said centerline, 160.00 feet to the projection of the East line of Block 2; thence S00°42'27"E along said East line, 210.00 feet to the South line of Block 2; thence S89°32'15"W along said South line, 320.00 feet to the true point of beginning, containing 2.50 acres more or less. ## Section 2:

A copy of the e ordinance duly certified by the city Recorder shall be filed in the Office of the Circuit Clerk and Ex-Officio Recorder of the County and recorder in the deed records for the Eastern District of Clay County.
