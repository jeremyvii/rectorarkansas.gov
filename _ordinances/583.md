**An ordinance to prevent the discharge of firearms in the City of Rector, and for other purposes**

Whereas, the Rector City Council has reviewed other cities regulations on the discharge of firearms, and

Whereas, the Rector City Council has authority for public peace and safety to establish parameters for police control, and

Whereas, it is determined the discharge of firearms shall be only exercised in accordance with the Ordinance.

Now therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, State of Arkansas, as follows:

## Section 1: Definitions.

A firearm under this ordinance shall only pertain to any weapon, which will, or is designed to be readily converted to expel a projectile by the action of an explosive, such as gun powders or similar.

## Section 2.

The discharge of a firearm shall be prohibited in the City of Rector. The following exceptions shall be allowed.

1. When the discharge or firing is being conducted by a duly certified law enforcement officer, when necessary in the performance of her or her duty.
1. When the discharge or firing is being conducted on any public or private rifle or pistol range, shooting gallery, approved for use by the City and Chief of Police for operation in accordance with applicable State and Federal law.
1. When the discharge is for functional testing by a qualified fun repair individual is performance of their occupation and in connection with safety equipment and apparatuses approved by the Chief of Police.
{: .ordinance-list-lower-alpha}

## Section 3: Violations.

Any violations of this ordinance shall be subject to a fine of not less than $150.00 nor more than $250.00 and shall be imposed for each violation, of which can be stacked and imposed by the discretion of the District Court Judge.

## Section 4:

All other ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict are hereby repealed.

Passed and approved this 1st day of July, 2024.

By: [signed]  
Mayor Shawn Brandon

Attested: Debi Boyd [signed]  
City Clerk
