**AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 480, AMENDING THE ZONING ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.**

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS, AS FOLLOWS:

That Ordinance No. 480, as amended, is amended as set out hereinbelow:

## Section 1:

The Zoning Ordinances of the City of Rector, Arkansas, should be and they hereby are amended so that the land hereinafter described is zoned Commercial C-1, to-wit:

That part of the Northeast Quarter of the Northwest Quarter of Section 26, Township 19 North, Range 7 East, more particularly described as follows: Beginning at the Northeast corner of the Northeast Quarter of the Northwest Quarter of Section 26, Township 19 North, Range 7 East, run thence West 837.3 feet to the point of beginning proper, run thence South 3 degrees 05 minutes West 378.2 feet, run thence North 45 degrees 31 minutes West 326.7 feet to Railroad right-of-way, run thence North 43 degrees 26 minutes East 204.6 feet to Section line, run thence East 112.6 feet to the point of beginning proper, less and except the right-of-way of U.S. Highway #49 crossing said tract. Also described as Lot Five (5) of the Northeast Quarter of the Northwest Quarter (NE ¼ NW ¼ ) as aforesaid, according to Laffler’s Survey of the Town of Rector, in the Eastern District of Clay County, Arkansas.

## SECTION 2:

All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed.

## SECTION 3:

The City Clerk is hereby directed to amend the official zoning map of the City of Rector, Arkansas, insofar as it relates to the lands described above so that the Zoning thereof shall be in accordance with the provisions of this ordinance.

## SECTION 4:

An emergency is declared to exist by the City Council within the City of Rector and the passage of this ordinance is necessary for the preservation of public health, maintaining safety and public peace of the citizens of Rector. The members of the City Council hereby unanimously and expressly dispense with the rule requiring the reading of an ordinance of three different dates before its passage thereof, and this ordinance shall take effect and be enforced from and after its passage.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 7th day of July, 2008.

By: Mayor, Ron Kemp

ATTEST: Lowell D. McKenney, City Recorder/Treasurer
