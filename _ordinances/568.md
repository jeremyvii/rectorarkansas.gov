**AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 440, SETTING FORTH VIOLATIONS OF SPEEDING, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY**

Whereas, the City Council of the City of Rector has reviewed various traffic citations and violation of the law that would constitute Hazardous Driving; and

Whereas, the City Council of the City of Rector would like to modify the existing ordinance to include language regarding excessive speed.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS:

## SECTION 1.

Section 1 of Ordinance No. 440 shall be amended and be stated as follows:

That any person who drives any vehicle on the streets, alleys, highways, parking lots, and other areas within the limits of the City of Rector, Arkansas, in such a manner as to indicate:

<ol type="a" style="list-style-type: none;">
  <li>a) either a negligent or careless disregard for the safety of persons or property, or</li>
  <li>b) drives in excessive speed</li>
</ol>

shall be deemed to be guilty of hazardous driving, and the same is hereby declared to be a misdemeanor.

## SECTION 2.

All other portions of Ordinance 440 shall remain in full effect.

## SECTION 3.

Any provision that is declared void or in violation of law, shall be declared severable and the other portions shall be in full force.

## EMERGENCY CLAUSE:

This Ordinance is necessary for the preservation of immediate need in the court system, for adherence to request by the court and municipal judge, and for immediate prosecution for needs within the City of Rector, this Ordinance shall be in full force and effect after its passage and publication.

PASSED AND APPROVED on this 5th day of April, 2021.

Mayor Teresa Roofe

City Clerk, Shelia Dale
