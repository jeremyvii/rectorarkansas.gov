An Ordinance Vacating a Street Beginning at the Southwest Corner of Lot 1 in Block 24, of Phillip's Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas, run thence due East along the South boundary line of Lots 1 and 2 to the West boundary line (right of way line) of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad (a/k/a Union Pacific Railroad) run thence Southwesterly along the West boundary line (right of way line) of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad to the Northeast Corner of Block 27, run thence due West along the North boundary line of Block 27, a distance of 125 feet to the Northwest Corner of Block 27, thence due North to the Southwest Corner of Block 1, which is the point of beginning, Authorizing the City Recorder to Record said Ordinance in the Deed Records of the County.

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows, to-wit:

Wherefore, a petition was duly filed with the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, on the [illegible] day of [illegible], 2005, asking the City Council to vacate and abandon all that portion of a street designated a street Beginning at the Southwest Corner of Lot 1 in Block 24, of Phillip’s Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas, run thence due East along the South boundary line of Lots 1 and 2 to the West boundary line (right of way line) of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad (a/k/a Union Pacific Railroad) run thence Southwesterly along the West boundary line (right of way line) of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad to the Northeast Corner of Block 27, run thence due West along the North boundary line of Block 27, a distance of 125 feet to the Northwest Corner of Block 27, thence due North to the Southwest Corner of Block 1, which is the point of beginning.

Whereas, after due notice as required by law, the City Council has, at the time and place mentioned in the notice, heard all persons desiring to be heard on the question and has ascertained that a street or the portion thereof, hereinbefore described, has heretofore been dedicated to the public use as a street herein described; that said street has not been actually used by the public generally for a period of at least five years subsequent to the filing of the plat; that all the owner of the property abutting upon the portion of a street to be vacated has filed with the Council his written consent to such abandonment; and the public interest and welfare will not be adversely affected by the abandonment of such street.

Now therefore,

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows, to-wit:

## Section 1.

The City of Rector, Arkansas, hereby releases, vacates and abandons all its rights, together with the rights of the public generally, in and to a street designated as follows:

Beginning at the Southwest Corner of Lot 1 in Block 24, of Phillip’s Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas, run thence due East along the South boundary line of Lots 1 and 2 to the West boundary line (right of way line) of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad (a/k/a Union Pacific Railroad) run thence Southwesterly along the West boundary line (right of way line) of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad to the Northeast Corner of Block 27, run thence due West along the North boundary line of Block 27, a distance of 125 feet to the Northwest Corner of Block 27, thence due North to the Southwest Corner of Block 1, which is the point of beginning.

## Section 2.

A copy of the ordinance duly certified by the City Recorder shall be filed in the Office of the Circuit Clerk and Ex-Officio Recorder of the county and recorder in the deed records for the Eastern District of Clay County.

## Section 3.

This ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage.

Passed and approved by the City Council of the City of Rector, this [illegible] day of [illegible], 2005.

Ron Kemp, Mayor

Attest: [illegible], City Clerk

State of Ark.

County of Clay

Acknowledge before me this 6th day of June 2005

Betty Benson

My Commission Expires 9-13-2005

## Supporting petition (PDF page 4)

### In the matter of the vacating of a street in the City of Rector, Arkansas located in the Phillip’s Addition

Comes now the petitioner, Michael Lindsey, by and through his attorney, C. Joseph Calvin, and for his relief requests that the City of Rector vacate a certain street within the city limits. The petitioner state that he is the sole abutting landowner on each side of the proposed property and that said street has not been used for a period of five years from the date of this petition.

Beginning at the Southwest Corner of Lot 1 in Block 24, of Phillip’s Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas, run thence due East along the South boundary line of Lots 1 and 2 to the West boundary line (right of way line) of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad (a/k/a Union Pacific Railroad) run thence Southwesterly along the West boundary line (right of way line) of the St. Louis Southwestern Railroad to the Northeast Corner of Block 27, run thence due West along the North boundary line of Block 27, a distance of 125 feet to the Northwest Corner of Block 27, thence due North to the Southwest Corner of Block 1, which is the point of beginning.

Wherefore, petitioner prays that he be granted an ordinance vacating the above described street and further that the ordinance be filed with the clerk of said county and that ownership of said street shall revert to the abutting land owner to the center of said street.
