**An ordinance vacating the street located in the Stafford Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas**

Whereas, the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, being in regular session on the 20th day of May, 2002, and a Petition being presented to the City Council by Eugene Martin, Norma Martin and Neda Sain, praying that the street abutting their property be vacated; and

Whereas, it appearing to the City Council that all proceedings have been had by the Petitioners in the County Court of Clay County, Arkansas, and that a hearing was held on the 20th day of May, 2002, whereby the County Court did enter and Order granting the relief prayed for by Petitioners and permitting the vacation of the street to begin.

Now therefore be it ordained, by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas:

## Section 1:

The following property is hereby vacated, to-wit:

All that part of the street lying West of and adjacent to Lot Ten (10) in Stafford’s Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas and being more particularly described as follows: Beginning at the Northwest corner of said Lot Ten (10) aforesaid, run thence South to the Southwest corner of said Lot Ten (10), run thence due West to a point on the East line of Lot Twelve (12) of Stafford’s Addition, run thence North to the Northeast corner of Lot Eleven (11), run thence East 50 feet, more or less, to the point of beginning.

## Section 2:

This Ordinance shall take effect and be in full force from and after its passage.

This ordinance passed and approved this 20th day of May, 2002.

City of Rector, Arkansas

By: Ron Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Kenneth Burns, Recorder/Treasurer
