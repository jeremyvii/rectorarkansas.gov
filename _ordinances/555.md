**An ordinance hereby authorizing the chief of police, district court clerk and administrative assistant of the City of Rector, and Rector Water Department office personnel, to accept and receipt court fines and costs for the citizens of Rector, Arkansas, and for other purposes.**

Be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas as follows:

## Section. 1.:

 In order to comply with audit procedures, and to better serve the citizens of the City of Rector, Arkansas, the City Council has determined that the Chief of Police, District Court Clerk and Administrative Assistance of the City of Rector, and the Rector Water Department Office Personnel, should be authorized to accept and receive court fines and costs for the citizens of Rector, Arkansas.

## Section 2:

It is determined that the City is in immediate need for such a policy for the citizens of the City of Rector, Arkansas; therefore, an emergency is declared to exist and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage, approval, and publication as required by law.

## Section 3:

All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed.

Passed and approved this 8 day of Sept., 2014
