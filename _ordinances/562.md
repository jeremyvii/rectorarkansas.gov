**An Ordinance to Amend the Zoning Ordinance of the City of Rector, Arkansas**

WHEREAS, under the authority of the zoning ordinance of the City of Rector, Arkansas, the following described lands are currently zoned “Industrial Use Zoning Classification:

Lot 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, & 12 of Block 30 of Harbert and Case Addition to the City of Rector, Arkansas.

WHEREAS, record title to the above described lands is owned by Michael and April Lindsay, and the rezoning of said lands to Central Business District “C-1” will promote the economy of the City and will conform to the City’s Land Use Plan; and

WHEREAS, the Rector Board of Adjustment has recommended the adoption of this Ordinance to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, it appears to the City Council that all applicable laws of the State of Arkansas and the City of Rector have been complied with in presenting this ordinance to the City Council.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, that:

## SECTION I.

Zoning Ordinance No. 359, as amended, of the City of Rector, Arkansas should be, and it hereby is, amended so that the above described lands are zoned “C-1” Central Business District Classification.

## SECTION II:

All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed.

## SECTION III:

The City Clerk is hereby directed to amend the official zoning map of the City of Rector, Arkansas, insofar as it relates to the lands described hereinabove so that zoning classification of said lands shall be in accordance with the provisions of this Ordinance

## SECTION IV.

The effective date of this ordinance shall be 30 (thirty) days following its publication.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 10th day of July, 2017.

CITY OF RECTOR

By: Teresa Roofe

Attested:

Shelia Dale, City Clerk
