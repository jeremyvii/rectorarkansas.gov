**An ordinance increasing city water and sewage rates for the City of Rector, Arkansas.**

Whereas, the City of Rector incurs great costs operating the water and sewer in the City of Rector and has not issued a cost of living increase in rates for several years and is therefore losing money and unable to update equipment to best serve the citizens of Rector; and

Whereas, it has been determined by the Rector City Council that a seven-percent (7%) increase is needed to bring the water and sewer account current and operate it efficiently; further that a two-percent (2%) increase per year for the next three (3) years would be sufficient to keep the account level and handle the depreciation in equipment; further, the deposit for water and sewer will be set at $100.00 for new residential customers.

Now therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkansas, as follows:

## Section 1:

Effective January 1, 2006, the water and sewer rates for the City of Rector will be increased by seven-percent (7%) and thereafter said rates will be increased by two-percent (2%) for the next three (3) years beginning January 1st of each year.

## Section 2:

Effective January 1, 2006, the water and sewer deposit for new residential customers will be set at $100.00.

## Section 3:

The actual rates are stated in the attached addendum.

Passed and approved this 7th day of November, 2005.

City of Rector

By: Ron Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Lowell McKenney, City Clerk/Treasurer
