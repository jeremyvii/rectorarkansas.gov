**"Entitled an ordinance to preserve the Sidewalks."**

"Be it ordained by the Town Council of the Incorporated Town of Rector."

## Sec. 1.

Any willful destruction of, or damage to any sidewalk in the Town of Rector, or the willful riding, driving or leading of any horse or other livestock upon, or across any sidewalk shall be a misdemeanor.

## Sec. 2.

Any violation of this ordinance shall be punished by a fine in any sum not to exceed Twenty-five dollars.

## Sec. 3.

This ordinance shall not be construed as prohibiting the crossing of sidewalks at points where they are intended by streets or alleys.

## Sec. 4.

This ordinance shall go into effect from and after its passage.

Signed: T. F. Trantham,  
Recorder

Signed: C. M. B Cox  
Mayor

Approved: November 6, 1897.
