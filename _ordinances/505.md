**AN ORDINANCE ALLOWING MAYOR RON KEMP TO BORROW AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $30,000.00 TO AID THE PURCHASE OF A NEW FIRE TRUCK AND FIRE STATION FOR THE CITY OF RECTOR.**

WHEREAS, the City of Rector needs funding for a new fire truck and fire station: and

WHEREAS, it will cost approximately $30.000.00 to complete purchase of a new fire truck and fire station: and

WHEREAS, Arkansas law allows the City of Rector to authorize the Mayor to enter into an agreement to obligate the City in borrowing funds to complete this kind of project as long as the note does not extend longer than a period of five (5) years. and

WHEREAS, the Rector City Council has voted to allow the Mayor to seek the funding with local area banks in order to secure the best interest rate available: and

WHEREAS, an emergency exists in which this Ordinance should take effect immediately so that the fire truck and fire station purchase can be completed.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF RECTOR, ARKANSAS:

## SECTION 1:

That Mayor Ron Kemp is hereby authorized to borrow up to $30.000.00 on behalf of the City of Rector with a local bank, with the best interest rate, for a lending period not to exceed five (5) years and said funds shall be used to aid in the purchase of a new fire truck and fire station.

## SECTION 2:

That an emergency is hereby declared to exist. in order to provide the City of Rector with the fire truck and fire station and thus provide for the health, safety and welfare of the community.

PASSED THIS 6th DAY OF DECEMBER, 2004.

ATTEST: KENNY BURNS, CITY CLERK

RON KEMP, MAYOR
