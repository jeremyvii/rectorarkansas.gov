**An ordinance entitled an ordinance to empower and authorize the City Council of the City of Rector to take over the management, control and operation of the Woodland Heights Cemetery and the Rector Memorial Park of the City of Rector, Arkansas; and for other purposes.**

Whereas, There is no outstanding bonded indebtness agaist Woodland Heights Cemetery, but there is an unpaid balance of Five Thousand Dollars ($5000.) against the Rector Memorial Park, secured by first mortgage payable to the Bank of Rector, Rector, Arkansas; the Cemetery and Memorial Park being maintain from proceeds from Labor Day picnic or donations.

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of the City of Rector, Arkan Arkansas,

## Section 1.

That the City of Rector, Arkansas, and the Council therof, hereby assume full power and authority, as provided by law, to operate and maintain the Woodland Heights Cemetery and the Rector Memorial Park, of the City of Rector, Arkansas, and does hereby assume full control and management of said Woodland Heights Cemetery and Rector Memorial Park.

## Section 2.

For the purpose of operating and maintaing said Woodland Heights Cemetery and Rector Memorial Park, of the City of Rector, Arkansas, a board of Managers is hereby created, composed of Five Members, to be appointed by the City Council, one for a term of one year, one for a term of two years, one for a term of three years, one for a term of four years and one appointed for a term of five years, and one member to be appointed annually thereafter for a term of five years; and all vacancies on said board to be filled by appointment for unexpired term. Said board of Managers shall serve without pay to themselves and are accountable to the City Council. They shall have power and authority to select a manager and treasure for said Woodland Heights Cemetery and Rector Memorial Park.

## Section 3.

The manager and treasure selected by said board of managers shall enter into bond to the City of Rector in sufficient sum to be determined by said board of managers, and City Council, and the board of managers and treasure making annual report to City Council showing receipt of all funds and expenditures of same, and for which expened, and the same to be published. Said bond to be with some approved bonding company as surety, for the faithful performance of his duty and the protection of all funds coming to his hands.

## Section 4.

That all ordinances and parts of Ordinances, in conflict with this Ordinance is hereby repealed, and this ordinance being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health and safety, an emergency is hereby declared to exist, and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after its approval and passage.

Passed and approved this 21st. day of October 1947.

Attest:-

Luther King, Recorder.

H. L. Alstadt, Mayor.
