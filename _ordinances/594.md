**An ordinance to amend Ordinance 372, 436, 359, and 447 for uniform manufactured and mobile home regulations for the City of Rector, Arkansas, and for other purposes**

**Whereas**, the City of Rector recognizes the importance of providing
safe, affordable, and attractive housing options, including manufactured
homes; and

**Whereas**, it is the desire of the Planning Commission and City
Council to ensure that all manufactured housing installed within the
City conforms to modern standards of appearance and safety consistent
with HUD regulations; and

**Whereas**, the City further desires to simplify, clarify, and unify
its manufactured home regulations for both R-1 and R-2 residential
zoning districts to ensure fairness, enforceability, and consistency;
and

**Whereas**, a public hearing was posted and was held with the Planning
Commission to allow public input, and

**Whereas,** the Planning Commission has made recommendations to the
City Council for modification of the existing ordinance.

**Now, therefore, be it ordained by the City Council of Rector,
Arkansas:**

## Section 1. The City Council of the City of Rector Arkansas shall establish Article 5-7

A new Article 5-7 of the Zoning Regulations of the City of Rector is
hereby adopted to read as follows:

### Article 5-7: Manufactured and Mobile Home Regulations

#### Purpose

To establish uniform standards for the placement, design,
and regulation of manufactured and mobile homes within all zoning
districts of the City of Rector, Arkansas.

#### Definitions

**Manufactured Home.** A dwelling unit manufactured in accordance with
the provisions set forth in 24CFR part 3280 Manufactured Home
Construction and Safety Standards Code as amended October 25, 1994, and
installed in accordance with applicable state regulations.

**Manufactured Housing Construction and Safety Standards Code.** Title
VI of the 1974 Housing and Community Development Act (42 U.S.C 540) et
sequentis), as amended (previously known as the federal Mobile Home
Construction and Safety Act), rules and regulations adopted thereunder
(including information supplied by the home manufacturer, which has been
stamped and approved by a Design Approval Primary Inspection Agency, and
agent of the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development pursuant
to HUD rules), which became effective for mobile/manufactured home
construction on June 15, 1976.

**Mobile Home.** A transportable structure having an undercarriage of
one or more axles and wheels, a hitch mechanism and designed to remain
capable of being towed on the highway. Merely placing such mobile home
on a permanent foundation does not alter its character so as to make it
a manufactured home for the purpose of this Ordinance.

**Mobile Home Park.** A commercial park developed for the purpose of
offering for rent or lease spaces for the location of mobile homes. Such
parks are equipped with utility connections, parking pads, and driveways
on each space, minimal interior street systems, and if designed for
twenty-five (25) or more mobile homes shall include a recreation area,
laundry and bathing facilities, and other amenities as determined by the
Planning Commission.

#### Permitted Units

Only manufactured homes bearing a HUD label shall be
permitted. No mobile home constructed prior to that date may be
installed or relocated within city limits. No manufactured home shall be
placed unless it was constructed within 20 years of the date of permit
application, as evidenced by its HUD certification label.

#### Installation Standards

All manufactured homes must:

Be a minimum of 14 feet in width and 750 square feet in enclosed floor
area.

Be supported by a permanent perimeter foundation constructed of concrete
or masonry, designed to transfer all loads to the ground, and extending
below the frost line. The use of block piers, runners, or other
temporary systems shall not satisfy this requirement.

Have all wheels, axles, and towing mechanisms removed.

Have utility hookups (water, sewer, electricity) completed and
inspected.

Be roofed with residential-style material and have a minimum roof pitch
of 3:12.

Have exterior siding made of vinyl, wood, fiber cement, or other
materials customarily used in site-built housing.

Be fully installed, including permanent foundation, removal of wheels
and towing apparatus, utility hookups, and required skirting, within 60
days of the home being delivered to the site.

The Code Enforcement Officer may grant one extension of up to 30
additional days upon written request showing good cause, including
weather delays, contractor availability, or other circumstances beyond
the applicant's control.

Failure to complete installation within the required time shall
constitute a violation of these regulations and be subject to
enforcement under Article 7-3.

#### Permitting Requirements

A Certificate of Zoning Compliance and Foundation Permit must be
obtained prior to installation.

Permit applications must include:

Site plan with setbacks.

Photographs or elevation drawings.

Foundation/skirting detail.

A copy of the approved manufacturer instructions, which will be used for
installation purposes.

Proof of HUD certification label affixed to the exterior of the home.

#### Petition Required for Manufactured Home Placement

All manufactured homes proposed to be placed on a private lot within any
zoning district, with the exception of Mobile Home Parks, shall require
a petition for placement to be submitted and approved prior to
installation.

The petition must include:

A written request to place a manufactured home on the parcel.

Photographs of the proposed unit.

A site plan showing setbacks, access, and placement.

Proof of HUD certification, as defined below.

Written consent from all adjacent property owners, as defined below.

##### Proof of HUD Label

Every manufactured home must display an intact HUD certification label
affixed to the exterior of the unit.

If the HUD label has been removed, defaced, or is otherwise not visible,
the applicant shall provide verification from HUD or the Institute for
Building Technology and Safety (IBTS) that the home was constructed in
compliance with federal standards.

No manufactured home shall be permitted without either (1) a visible HUD
label, or (2) official written verification from HUD/IBTS.

Adjacent property owners shall include all owners of parcels that abut
or touch the subject parcel on any side, including across any public
street, alley, or right-of-way.

If the petition is complete and compliant with all provisions of this
Article, the Code Enforcement Officer may approve the placement.

If the unit does not meet the requirements of this Article, the
applicant may seek a variance through the Board of Zoning Adjustment.

No manufactured home shall be installed, delivered, or connected to
utilities prior to receiving approval under this section.

#### Enforcement

Any installation or relocation of a manufactured home in
violation of these standards shall be subject to enforcement under
Article 7 of these regulations.

#### Supersession

This article shall govern the regulation of
manufactured and mobile homes in all zoning districts. Any other
provision of these zoning regulations in conflict with this article is
hereby superseded.

## Section 2. The City Council of the City of Rector shall repeal Article 2-1.C of Ordinance 372 and the following language shall be added at the end of Article 5-2.B of Ordinance 436

> "Manufactured homes shall be permitted only in accordance with Article
> 5-7 of these regulations."

All other references to manufactured homes within Article 5-2 of
Ordinance 436 are hereby repealed.

## Section 3. The City Council of the City of Rector shall add the following language at the end of Article 5-3.A.11 of Ordinance 372:

> "Manufactured homes shall be permitted only in accordance with Article
> 5-7 of these regulations."

All other references to manufactured or mobile homes in Article 5-3.B
are hereby repealed.

## Section 4. The City Council of the City of Rector shall repeal subsection C of Article 2-1 of Ordinance 372.

Its content is now incorporated into Article 5-7 adopted herein.

## Section 5. Severability

That the provisions of this Ordinance are separable and if a section,
phrase or provision shall be declared invalid, such declaration shall
not affect the validity of the remainder of the Ordinance.

## Section 6.

That all ordinances and resolutions and parts thereof in conflict
herewith are hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.

## Emergency clause

It is hereby declared that an emergency exists to preserve the public
peace, health, and safety of the citizens of Rector, Arkansas, by
ensuring consistent and enforceable housing regulations. Therefore, this
ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its passage and
publication.
