**An ordinance to regulate the use of skateboards, roller blades, roller skates, bicycles, manually powered scooters, and similar conveyances on the herein described city property.**

Whereas; The City Council has received numerous complaints concerning the use of skateboards, roller blades, roller skates, bicycles, manually powered scooters and similar conveyances on city property.

Whereas; The City Council has decided that it would be in the best interest of the citizens of the City that the use of skateboards, roller blades, roller skates, bicycles, manually powered scooters and similar conveyances be prohibited on certain property owned by the City.

Therefore, effective December 1, 2002, all individuals are hereby prohibited from the use of the following instruments, as described herein; skateboards, roller blades, roller skates, bicycles, manually powered scooters and similar conveyances, on the below described property owned by the City;

1. North of railroad track to the north boundary of Second Street including Main Street and all sidewalks thereon. Including:

   1. The sidewalks of Fifth Street west to the point at which Fifth Street intersects Dodd Street and east to the point that Fifth Street runs into Stewart Street.

   2. The sidewalks of Front Street.

   3. The sidewalks of Stewart Street from the point at which Stewart Street begins progressing north to the point at which Stewart Street intersects Second Street.

   4. The sidewalks of Fourth Street west to the point at which Fourth Street intersects Dodd Street and east to the point at which Fourth Street intersects Stewart Street.

   5. The sidewalks of Third Street west to the point at which Third Street intersects Dodd Street and east to the point at which Third Street intersects Stewart Street.

   6. The sidewalks of Second Street west to the point at which Second Street intersects Dodd Street and east to the point at which Second Street intersects Stewart Street.
   {: .ordinance-list-decimal}
{: .ordinance-list-upper-alpha}

## Section 1:

Violation of this ordinance shall be a misdemeanor and the fine for each violation shall be a minimum of twenty-five dollars ($25.00) and a maximum of one hundred dollars ($100.00) and each day of such violation will be considered as a separate offense.

## Section 2:

It has been determined that this ordinance is necessary and is vital to the health, welfare and safety of the public, therefore, an emergency is hereby declared to exist and this ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage and approval.

Ronald Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Kenneth R. Burns, City Clerk-Recorder
