Whereas, the City Council of the City of Rector, being in session this 15th day of December, 2008, and,

Whereas, the Council, having been duly advised that the City should, pursuant to Arkansas Law, adopt an amended budget for 2008, and,

Whereas, the City Council has been presented a 2008 amended budget for the General Fund, Street Fund, and other funds of the City of Rector, and that a copy of the 2008 Amended budget as affixed hereto and marked Exhibit “a” and made part of this Ordinance as though set forth word for word herein, and,

Whereas, the Council carefully examined the 2008 Amended Budget and after motion having been duly made and seconded and voted on by the Rector City Council, the 2008 Amended Budget is hereby declared the subject of the following findings:

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the governmental body of the City of Rector, Arkansas that the 2008 Amended Budget, which is in words and terms as set forth in the attached Exhibit “a” for the General Fund, Street Fund, and other funds of the City of Rector is hereby adopted this 15th day of December, 2008.

## (Emergency clause)

Because of immediate need to provide definition and permitted use of the 2008 Amended Budget, an emergency is declared to exist and the Ordinance shall be in force as of the date of its passage and first publication.

Passed and approved this 15th day of December, 2008.

City of Rector, Arkansas

Ronald E. Kemp, Mayor

Attest: Lowell D. McKenney, Recorder/Treasurer
