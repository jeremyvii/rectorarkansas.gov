**An ordinance to amend Ordinance No. 307 Section 2 to revise the amount of the fine to owners for allowing their dog or dogs to run at large**

Whereas: It has come to the attention of the City of Rector Council Members that it would be in the best interest of the citizens of Rector if the minimum fine and maximum fine to owners who let their dog or dogs run at large be revised. The current fine is a minimum of Five Dollars ($5.00) and a maximum of Twenty Five Dollars ($25.00).

Now, therefore, be it ordained by the City of Rector Council Members as follows, to wit:

That Ordinance No. 307 Section 2 is hereby amended to revise the amount of the fine to the owners for allowing their dogs to run at large. The minimum fine shall be $50.00 and the maximum fine shall be $100.00.

## Section 2.

That any owner who allows their dog or dogs to run at large in the corporate limits of the City of Rector shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction shall be subject to a fine not less than Fifty Dollars ($50.00) nor more than One Hundred Dollars ($100.00) and each day that said dog is allowed to continue to run at large shall be considered a separate offense and punishable as such.

## Section 3:

This ordinance being necessary for the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Rector and necessary for effective meeting, there being a significant scheduling problem for members of the City Council within the City of Rector, an emergency is declared to exist and this ordinance shall take effect and being in force and after its passage and publication.

Passed and approved this 4th day of May, 1998.

Mayor Ronald E. Kemp

Attest: Recorder/Treasurer Kenneth R. Burns
